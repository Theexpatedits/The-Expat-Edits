OUR BANGKOK

A city that keeps pulling you out into the street.

Bangkok is one of those cities where the best thing to do is simply get out and explore. We stayed at the Pullman, which gave us a comfortable base, but most of our favourite memories happened away from the hotel.

We loved following the river, eating and drinking beside it, taking boats through the city and watching Bangkok change from day to night. The temples, markets and streets give every outing a different feel.

And then there are the evenings — rooftop drinks, Asiatique, good food and the sort of unexpected moments you could never really plan for.

FINDING OUR BASE

Somewhere comfortable to come back to.

We stayed at the Pullman, which worked particularly well for us as a family. After a day of traffic, temples, markets and wandering, it was good to have somewhere comfortable to return to — and the rooftop bar gave us another reason to stay around for the evening.

THE RIVER

Follow the water.

Travelling along the river is one of our favourite ways to see Bangkok. Boats make getting around part of the experience, and the view constantly changes between temples, hotels, homes, restaurants and the huge energy of the city.

BY THE WATER

Eat, drink and watch Bangkok go by.

Some of our favourite Bangkok evenings have been spent along the river. There is something about sitting down for food or a drink with the city moving around you that captures Bangkok perfectly.

THE CITY

There is always another corner to discover.

Bangkok rewards curiosity. We love the temples, the markets, the streets and the fact that an ordinary walk can suddenly turn into something memorable.

THE TEMPLES

Some of the city's most beautiful moments.

We love Bangkok's temples. They give the city a completely different rhythm from the traffic and towers, and they are one of the reasons exploring Bangkok never feels repetitive.

FROM THE JOURNAL

Bangkok is a city to explore.

What we like about Bangkok is that it never really asks you to follow a plan. You can leave the hotel intending to see one thing and end up somewhere completely different, usually with a much better story to tell.

For the family, that meant mixing the obvious highlights with simple moments. The boys playing pool with local people, travelling along the river, stopping for food, wandering around Asiatique and discovering another temple all became part of the experience.

And Bangkok works particularly well at night. We loved the Pullman rooftop, the Lebua State Tower and the restaurants and bars along the river. There is a real sense that the city continues long after the heat of the day has disappeared.

The Experience

Bangkok with the family

Bangkok is one of those cities that can feel completely overwhelming at first, but travelling as a family gave us a different way of experiencing it.

We stayed at the Pullman, which turned out to be a very good base for us. The rooms and family-friendly room options made the stay particularly easy, giving everyone enough space to relax after a day in the city.

One of our favourite evenings was spent at Asiatique. As the sun went down, the riverside came alive with restaurants, bars, lights and plenty going on. It was an easy place to spend an evening together without needing to plan too much.
