OUR BANGKOK

Bangkok

A city to explore.

OUR BANGKOK

A city that keeps pulling you out into the street.

Bangkok is one of those cities where the best thing to do is simply get out and explore. We stayed at the Pullman, which gave us a comfortable and well-located base for our family city break.

We loved following the river, taking boats through the city and visiting the temples along the way. It is a wonderful way to see Bangkok, with the scenery constantly changing as you travel.

And then there are the evenings. We loved strolling around Asiatique, enjoying the rooftop bars and discovering the different sides of a city that seems to have something happening around every corner.

THE STAY

Pullman Bangkok.

A comfortable, well-located base that worked particularly well for us as a family.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A good base for exploring the city.

The Pullman was a good choice for us. Its location worked well for getting out and exploring Bangkok, while still giving us somewhere comfortable to return to at the end of a busy day.

The family room product worked particularly well. Having the right room arrangement made the stay easy and comfortable for all of us, which makes a real difference on a city break.

The rooftop bar was another plus. After a day spent exploring the temples, streets and river, it was a lovely place to settle in for a drink and enjoy the city from above.

For a family wanting a well-located hotel, a practical room arrangement and somewhere to enjoy an evening drink, the Pullman worked very well for us.

AFTER DARK

Bangkok takes on a different energy in the evening.

We enjoyed the city's rooftop bars and the atmosphere of its restaurants and streets once the evening arrived. It is a side of Bangkok that is well worth making time for.

The Pullman's rooftop gave us an easy place to enjoy a drink, while the wider city offered plenty of reasons to head out again.

THE EXPERIENCE

See Bangkok from the river.

One of our favourite ways to explore the city was simply to get on a boat and follow the water.

LIFE ALONG THE WATER

Let the journey be part of the day.

Travelling along the river is one of the things we enjoyed most about Bangkok. We used the boats to visit the temples, but the journey itself was every bit as enjoyable.

The view changes constantly. Temples, buildings, riverside restaurants and everyday city life all appear along the banks, giving you a different perspective from the busy streets.

It is an easy way to make getting around part of the experience rather than simply a means of reaching the next place.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering Bangkok.

THE TEMPLES

A different rhythm to the city.

Bangkok's temples are among the places we most enjoyed exploring. They offer a complete change of pace from the traffic and movement of the modern city, with beautiful architecture and details to discover at every turn.

THE DETAIL

Look a little closer.

Some of the most interesting moments are found in the details: the decoration, the colours, the craftsmanship and the scale of the architecture around you.

It is worth slowing down and taking the time to look beyond the main buildings.

EXPLORING

Every corner has something to see.

We enjoyed wandering through the temple grounds, taking in the different buildings and discovering the details that make each part of the experience distinctive.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Beautiful from every angle.

The temples are full of colour, intricate decoration and striking shapes. Even looking up at the rooftops and spires reveals another layer of detail.

BEYOND BANGKOK

A family experience in Hua Hin.

Our elephant visit was not in Bangkok itself. We travelled to a sanctuary in Hua Hin, where we had the opportunity to spend time around these remarkable animals.

It was a very different experience from the city and a memorable addition to our time in Thailand.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Making the most of a Bangkok city break.

Bangkok is a city that rewards getting out and exploring. For us, a comfortable hotel base, time on the river and a few evenings set aside for wandering made for a great family trip.

WHERE TO STAY

Choose a good base

We stayed at the Pullman and found it well located for our trip. The family room arrangement worked particularly well, and the rooftop bar was a welcome bonus.

GETTING AROUND

Take to the river

We loved travelling by boat to visit the temples. It is a memorable way to see another side of Bangkok and makes the journey part of the experience.

EVENINGS

Make time for Asiatique

We particularly enjoyed strolling around Asiatique at night. It is an easy way to spend an evening enjoying the atmosphere and seeing the city after dark.

TEMPLE VISITS

Allow time to explore

The temples were a highlight for us. Rather than rushing between places, leave time to walk around and appreciate the architecture and detail.

BEYOND THE CITY

Consider Hua Hin

Our elephant sanctuary visit was in Hua Hin, not Bangkok. It made for a different family experience beyond the city itself.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Families and city explorers

Bangkok worked well for us as a family city break, with a mix of temples, boat journeys, food, evening exploring and plenty of variety.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Pullman

Our hotel base for the trip. Well located, comfortable for the family and with a family room product that worked particularly well for us. The rooftop bar was another plus.

02

Asiatique

One of our favourite places to spend an evening. We loved strolling around after dark and enjoying the atmosphere.

03

The Chao Phraya River

Take a boat and make the journey part of the day. Travelling along the river was one of our favourite ways to see the city and visit the temples.

04

Bangkok's temples

A highlight of our time in the city. Beautiful architecture, intricate details and a completely different atmosphere from the streets outside.

05

Lebua State Tower

The rooftop bar was another of the evening experiences we enjoyed. Bangkok is an impressive city to see from above as the lights come on.

06

Hua Hin elephant sanctuary

A memorable experience beyond Bangkok. Our visit was in Hua Hin and offered a different kind of family day out.

OUR EDIT

Take the river, explore the temples and stay out after dark.

What we like about Bangkok is that it never really asks you to follow a plan. You can head out intending to see one thing and find yourself somewhere completely different.

For us, the river was one of the best ways to explore. We loved travelling by boat to visit the temples, taking in the city from the water and then spending our evenings wandering around Asiatique.

The Pullman worked well as a family base, and having a comfortable room and a good rooftop bar made the stay easy. Our visit to the elephant sanctuary in Hua Hin added another memorable experience beyond the city.

Bangkok is a city that rewards curiosity. Get out, explore and leave a little room for the unexpected.
