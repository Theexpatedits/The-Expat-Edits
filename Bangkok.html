OUR BANGKOK

Bangkok

A city of contrasts, best explored by river.

OUR BANGKOK

A city that keeps surprising you.

Bangkok is a city of contrasts. Ancient temples sit alongside modern towers, quiet courtyards give way to busy streets, and the river offers a completely different perspective on the city.

We came as a family and found it a brilliant city to explore. Some of our favourite days were spent travelling by boat along the Chao Phraya, stopping to visit temples and taking in the life that unfolds along the riverbanks.

We stayed at the Pullman, which worked particularly well for us as a family. Between the rooftop bar, river journeys, evening markets and a few memorable surprises along the way, Bangkok gave us plenty to remember.

THE STAY

Pullman Bangkok.

A well-located base for exploring the city, with a family room that worked very well for us.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A good base for a family city break.

We stayed at the Pullman and were very happy with our choice. Its location made it a convenient base for getting out and exploring Bangkok, without feeling that we were spending the whole trip travelling between places.

The family room worked particularly well for us. Having enough space for everyone makes a real difference on a city break, especially when you are travelling with children.

The rooftop bar was another highlight. It was a lovely place to come back to after a day out, take in the city skyline and enjoy a drink while Bangkok carried on below us.

Our verdict: a practical and comfortable family choice, well located for exploring Bangkok and with a rooftop bar that we enjoyed.

THE CITY

A skyline that never stands still.

One of the things we enjoyed about Bangkok was the contrast between its different neighbourhoods. There is always something happening, whether you are looking down from above or making your way through the streets.

It is a city that rewards getting out and exploring rather than staying in one place.

THE CHAO PHRAYA

Bangkok is best seen from the water.

Travelling along the river became one of our favourite parts of the trip. It is an easy and enjoyable way to reach many of the city's most famous sights.

LIFE ON THE RIVER

A different view of the city.

We loved using the river boats to get around. The journey itself becomes part of the experience, passing temples, riverside buildings, local boats and the everyday activity along the banks.

It also gives you a welcome break from the traffic. From the water, Bangkok feels more open and relaxed, even when the streets are busy.

We would definitely recommend making the river part of your plans rather than treating it simply as a way to get from one attraction to another.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering Bangkok.

THE TEMPLES

A city rich in history.

Bangkok's temples are among its most memorable sights. Their intricate details, golden decoration and quiet courtyards offer a striking contrast to the pace of the surrounding city.

We loved taking the time to explore them, rather than rushing from one attraction to the next.

THE DETAILS

Look a little closer.

Some of the most interesting moments came from the details: the decoration, the statues and the architecture that make each temple feel different.

There is plenty to take in, and the quieter corners can be just as rewarding as the best-known viewpoints.

THE CONTRAST

Old Bangkok and the modern city.

What makes Bangkok so interesting is the way its different worlds sit side by side. Traditional buildings and temples are never far from the traffic, towers and energy of the modern city.

OUT AND ABOUT

Take the city as it comes.

Bangkok is a place to wander, stop when something catches your eye and enjoy the unexpected.

We found that some of the best moments came when we were not following a particularly strict plan.

AFTER DARK

An evening at Asiatique.

RIVERSIDE EVENINGS

A lovely way to spend an evening.

We particularly enjoyed strolling around Asiatique at night. Set beside the river, it has a lively atmosphere, with plenty to look at and somewhere to stop for something to eat or drink.

It was one of those easy family evenings where there was no real need for a plan. We could simply wander, enjoy the lights and take in a different side of Bangkok.

We also made time for the city's rooftop bars, including the famous Lebua State Tower. Seeing Bangkok from above is another experience worth making time for.

BEYOND BANGKOK

A family experience in Hua Hin.

Not part of the city itself, but one of the experiences from our wider Thailand trip that deserves a place in the story.

THE ELEPHANT SANCTUARY

A memorable day with elephants.

Our elephant encounter took place at a sanctuary in Hua Hin, rather than in Bangkok itself.

It was a lovely family experience and something different from the temples, river journeys and city exploring that made up the Bangkok part of our trip.

We wanted to include it here as part of the wider family travel story, while keeping clear that Hua Hin was a separate excursion beyond the city.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

How to make the most of Bangkok.

Bangkok can feel busy at first, but a little planning makes it a very rewarding city to explore. These are a few thoughts from our trip.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

November – February

The cooler, drier months are generally the most comfortable time to explore Bangkok. Temperatures remain warm, but the humidity is usually more manageable.

WEATHER

Hot and humid

Bangkok is hot for much of the year. Plan outdoor sightseeing for earlier in the day where possible, take breaks and keep water with you.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Three to five days

We would allow at least three days to explore the temples, river and different neighbourhoods, with longer if you want to take things at a more relaxed pace.

GETTING AROUND

Use the river

The Chao Phraya boats are a great way to reach riverside sights and enjoy another perspective on the city. Combine them with other transport depending on where you are going.

WHAT NOT TO MISS

Temples and street food

Make time for the temples, explore the river and try the local food. Bangkok's street food and markets are a big part of its character.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Curious families and city explorers

Bangkok is a great city break for families who enjoy culture, food, exploring and a little adventure. There is plenty to keep everyone interested.

OUR EDIT

A city best explored at your own pace.

Bangkok was a brilliant family city break. We enjoyed the contrast between the temples and the modern city, and found that travelling along the river was one of the best ways to experience it.

Pullman worked well for us as a base, the family room made the stay comfortable and the rooftop bar gave us somewhere to relax at the end of the day. We loved the evenings around Asiatique and the chance to see Bangkok from above at Lebua.

One of our favourite memories was an unplanned moment when the boys ended up playing pool with some local people. It was a small thing, but exactly the sort of unexpected experience that makes travelling as a family so enjoyable.

Our advice is to give Bangkok a few days, make the river part of your plans and leave room for the unexpected.
