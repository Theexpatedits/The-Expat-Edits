The Experience

Our Singapore

Singapore is different for us because it isn't simply somewhere we have travelled to. It is home.

Since moving to Singapore, we have discovered a side of the city that goes far beyond its famous skyline. Tropical greenery is everywhere, wildlife appears in the most unexpected places and the waterfront becomes part of everyday life.

What we love most is the contrast. One moment we're surrounded by skyscrapers and the energy of the city, and the next we're watching monitor lizards by the water, discovering a snake in the trees or picking bananas that have grown in our own garden.

These are the moments that have made Singapore feel like home to us.

The City

Singapore's skyline is one of the most recognisable in Asia, but living here changes the way you see it. The towers, waterfront and spectacular architecture become part of the landscape of everyday life rather than simply something to photograph.

02

Life By The Water

Some of our favourite Singapore moments are the ones that simply happen here. Watching our son DJ by the waterfront is one of them — a reminder that this isn't just somewhere we visit, but somewhere our family is building its own memories.

03

Our Tropical Garden

One of the things we love about Singapore is how easily tropical life becomes part of everyday life. These bananas grow in our own garden, which still feels like a pretty special thing to say about the place we now call home.
