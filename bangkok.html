THAILAND

Bangkok

A family city break of energy, lights and riverside evenings.

The Experience

Bangkok with the family

Bangkok is one of those cities that can feel completely overwhelming at first, but travelling as a family gave us a different way of experiencing it.

We stayed at the Pullman, which turned out to be a very good base for us. The rooms and family-friendly room options made the stay particularly easy, giving everyone enough space to relax after a day in the city.

One of our favourite evenings was spent at Asiatique. As the sun went down, the riverside came alive with restaurants, bars, lights and plenty going on. It was an easy place to spend an evening together without needing to plan too much.

01

Finding our base

Having somewhere comfortable to come back to makes a difference when you're travelling with children. The Pullman worked well for us, particularly because of the room options available for families.

02

Into the city

Bangkok is a city where there is always something happening. The streets, buildings, traffic and people create an energy that makes even a simple afternoon feel like part of the experience.

03

Asiatique after dark

Asiatique was one of the places we enjoyed most. Once evening arrived, the riverside filled with lights, restaurants and bars. It had enough atmosphere to keep everyone entertained and was an easy place to spend a relaxed family evening.

Our Take

Bangkok works brilliantly as a family city break.

Bangkok can be intense, but having a comfortable base and keeping the days relatively uncomplicated made it an enjoyable family trip for us. The Pullman gave us somewhere easy to return to, while evenings at Asiatique provided one of the most relaxed highlights of the stay.
