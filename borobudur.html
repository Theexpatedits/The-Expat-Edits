The Experience

Sunrise at Borobudur

Watching the first light break over Borobudur is one of Indonesia's most unforgettable experiences. As mist rolls across the Kedu Plain and the surrounding volcanoes emerge, the ancient temple slowly comes to life. It is a place where history, architecture and spirituality combine in extraordinary fashion.
