CAMBODIA

Cambodia

Temples, Siem Reap and unforgettable evenings.

The Journey

More than just Angkor

Cambodia had been on our list for a while, and Angkor Wat was obviously the main reason for coming. Seeing it at sunrise was every bit as special as we'd hoped.

But it was Siem Reap that really surprised us. We loved the relaxed atmosphere during the day and the energy that comes alive around the markets and Pub Street in the evening.

Add a superb family-run hotel, some great places to eat and a few excellent cocktail bars, and we found ourselves enjoying the town just as much as the temples.

01

Angkor at sunrise

Angkor Wat at sunrise is the obvious highlight of any first visit to Cambodia, and it absolutely deserves its reputation.

Getting there early, watching the light come up behind the temple and then exploring the enormous complex afterwards made for a memorable start to the trip.

Siem Reap

A town worth staying for

The great thing about Siem Reap is that once you've finished exploring the temples, there is still plenty to enjoy.

We loved wandering around the markets and discovering the streets around Pub Street. During the day it feels relaxed, but as the evening arrives the whole place becomes much more lively.

Eat & Drink

Good food, great nights

The food scene around Siem Reap was another pleasant surprise.

Lost City Pizza became a particular favourite, while Elia Greek near the market was another place we'd happily return to.

For cocktails, we'd recommend Miss Wong. It's the sort of intimate bar that makes a great stop after dinner before heading back out into the streets.

Where We Stayed

Navutu Dreams

Navutu Dreams was superb. It is a family-run hotel and has the sort of personal feel that is increasingly difficult to find.

The setting is peaceful and the hotel made a perfect base after busy days exploring Angkor. We would happily recommend it.

Another Option

Raffles

Raffles is another excellent option if you want something more classic and luxurious.

We particularly loved the enormous swimming pool. Even if you don't stay there, it is worth seeing and captures the old-world character that makes the hotel such an appealing alternative.

Our Take

Come for Angkor. Stay for Siem Reap.

Angkor Wat may be the reason to make the journey, but Siem Reap gives you plenty of reasons to stay. We loved the combination of incredible temples, a relaxed hotel, good food and lively evenings. It is a place we'd happily return to.
