Ancient temples, jungle sunrises and timeless Khmer history combine to create one of the world's greatest archaeological wonders.

The Experience

Where history comes alive

Few places have left an impression on us quite like Angkor. Watching the first rays of sunlight illuminate Angkor Wat before the crowds arrive is one of those travel moments that stays with you long after you've returned home.

While Angkor Wat may be the headline attraction, it's only one chapter of the story. Exploring the smiling faces of Bayon, wandering through Ta Prohm as giant tree roots engulf ancient stone walls and discovering hidden temples deep within the jungle makes every day feel like an adventure.

Based just outside Siem Reap, Angkor offers far more than remarkable architecture. The warmth of the Cambodian people, bustling local markets and slower pace of life create a destination that feels every bit as memorable as the temples themselves.

01

Sunrise at Angkor Wat

There's a reason people wake before dawn for this. As the sky slowly changes colour behind the iconic towers of Angkor Wat, the ancient temple emerges from the darkness to reveal one of the most spectacular sunrises anywhere in the world.

02

The Faces of Bayon

Hidden within the ancient city of Angkor Thom, Bayon is unlike any temple we've visited. Hundreds of enormous stone faces watch silently from every direction, creating an atmosphere that's both peaceful and unforgettable.

03

Nature Reclaims History

At Ta Prohm, towering jungle trees have wrapped themselves around centuries-old temple walls, creating one of Angkor's most iconic sights. It's a reminder that nature and history can exist beautifully side by side.
