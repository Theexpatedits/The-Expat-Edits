OUR CAMBODIA

Come for the temples. Stay for Siem Reap.

Cambodia was somewhere we'd wanted to visit for a while, and Angkor Wat was obviously a big part of the reason for going. But what surprised us most was just how much we enjoyed Siem Reap itself. It turned out to be a superb city break, with incredible temples, great food, markets and plenty happening once the sun went down.

We stayed at Navutu Dreams, a small boutique hotel which turned out to be an excellent choice for what we wanted. It was peaceful and relaxed after busy days exploring, and ultimately we were very pleased we'd chosen it. We visited Raffles for lunch too, which was pleasant and has a huge pool, but for us Navutu was the better fit.

The temples were every bit as magical as we'd hoped. Angkor Wat is extraordinary, but we also loved exploring Bayon Temple and the wider temple complex. There is something special about moving between the ruins and imagining the enormous civilisation that once occupied this landscape.

Back in Siem Reap, we loved wandering through the markets and exploring around Pub Street. We found Madam Fan for drinks and Lost City Pizza for a great meal — exactly the sort of places that made the evenings feel relaxed, lively and fun.

The combination of extraordinary temples, a great little hotel and a town that is genuinely enjoyable to explore made Cambodia one of those trips that worked perfectly as a short break.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Cambodia, as we saw it.

BEST TIME

November – February

The cooler, drier months are particularly enjoyable for exploring the temples and spending time walking around Siem Reap.

WEATHER

Tropical

Hot for much of the year, with a distinct wet season. The greener months can be beautiful too, but the heat is something to plan around.

GETTING AROUND

Tuk-tuk & walk

Siem Reap is easy to explore, while a tuk-tuk makes getting between the town and the temples simple and enjoyable.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Angkor Wat

The obvious reason to come, and it completely lives up to the expectation. Give yourself time to explore beyond the famous views and appreciate the scale of the whole complex.

02

Bayon Temple

One of our favourites within the wider Angkor complex. The faces and extraordinary architecture make it one of those places that stays with you.

03

Navutu Dreams

A small boutique hotel and an excellent choice for us. Peaceful, relaxed and a great place to come back to after exploring the temples.

04

Raffles

We visited for lunch and enjoyed it. The huge swimming pool is particularly impressive, although ultimately we were happier with our choice of Navutu.

05

Madam Fan

A bar we discovered in Siem Reap and a great place to head for a drink while exploring the town.

06

Lost City Pizza

A surprisingly good find and exactly the sort of relaxed place we enjoy discovering when we're away. Worth putting on the list.

OUR EDIT

Don't just see the temples.

Angkor Wat may be the reason you book the flight, but we'd give yourself time to enjoy Siem Reap too. The markets, restaurants, bars and streets around Pub Street give the trip a completely different rhythm once you're back from the temples.

For us, the combination was what made the trip work: extraordinary history during the day, then a relaxed hotel and lively evenings in town. Cambodia is much more enjoyable when you give yourself time for both.
