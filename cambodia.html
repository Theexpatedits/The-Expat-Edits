Cambodia

Temples, Siem Reap & unforgettable evenings.

OUR CAMBODIA

Come for the temples. Stay for Siem Reap.

Cambodia was somewhere we'd wanted to visit for a while, and Angkor Wat was obviously a big part of the reason for going. But what surprised us most was just how much we loved Siem Reap itself.

It turned out to be a superb short break — great temples during the day, a relaxed hotel to come back to, and lively evenings filled with markets, restaurants, bars and the streets around Pub Street.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Cambodia, as we saw it.

ANGKOR

Extraordinary, even when you've seen the pictures.

Angkor Wat completely lived up to the expectation. Seeing it in person — particularly around sunrise — makes you appreciate the enormous scale of the civilisation that created it.

THE TEMPLES

Angkor is so much more than one temple.

Angkor Wat may be the obvious headline, but exploring the wider complex is where the trip really comes alive. Bayon Temple was one of our favourites, with its extraordinary faces and sense of scale.

BEYOND ANGKOR

Give yourself time to explore.

The ruins, trees and ancient walls constantly change as you move through the complex. It is easy to spend the whole day exploring and still feel as though you've only scratched the surface.

SIEM REAP

We loved the town as much as the temples.

This was perhaps our biggest surprise. Siem Reap has an easy, relaxed energy and is genuinely enjoyable once you're back from the temples. We loved wandering through the markets, heading towards Pub Street and seeing where the evening took us.

NAVUTU DREAMS

The perfect place to come back to.

We stayed at Navutu Dreams and thought it was superb. Peaceful, relaxed and with a boutique feel, it was a great contrast to the heat and activity of the temples. For us, it was exactly the right place to stay.

BEST TIME

November – February

The cooler, drier months are particularly enjoyable for exploring the temples and spending time walking around Siem Reap.

WEATHER

Hot & tropical

Cambodia is hot for much of the year, with a distinct wet season. The greener months can be beautiful too, but the heat is something to plan around.

GETTING AROUND

Tuk-tuk & walk

Siem Reap is easy to explore, while a tuk-tuk makes getting between the town and the temples simple, inexpensive and enjoyable.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Angkor Wat

The obvious reason to come — and it completely lives up to the expectation. Give yourself time to explore beyond the famous views and appreciate the scale of the whole complex.

02

Bayon Temple

One of our favourites within the wider Angkor complex. The extraordinary faces and architecture make it one of those places that stays with you.

03

Navutu Dreams

A small boutique hotel and an excellent choice for us. Peaceful, relaxed and a great place to come back to after exploring the temples.

04

Sokkhak River Restaurant

One of the places we really enjoyed eating in Siem Reap. A lovely setting and exactly the sort of relaxed evening we were looking for after a day exploring.

05

Pub Street & the night market

We loved heading into town in the evening. The night market, Pub Street and the surrounding streets gave Siem Reap a lively atmosphere without making it feel too overwhelming.

06

Lost City Pizza

A surprisingly good find and exactly the sort of relaxed place we enjoy discovering when we're away. Worth putting on the list.

07

The night market

One of the fun parts of exploring Siem Reap after dark. And yes, this was also where we found ourselves eating tarantulas — not something we expected to be doing when we booked the trip.

08

Raffles

We visited for lunch and enjoyed it, particularly the enormous swimming pool. It was a great place to see, although ultimately we were happier with our choice of Navutu Dreams.

OUR EDIT

Don't just see the temples.

Angkor Wat may be the reason you book the flight, but we'd give yourself time to enjoy Siem Reap too. The markets, restaurants, bars and streets around Pub Street give the trip a completely different rhythm once you're back from the temples.

For us, the combination was what made the trip work: extraordinary history during the day, a superb little hotel to come back to, and relaxed, lively evenings in town. Even eating tarantulas somehow became part of the story.

Cambodia ended up being one of those trips where the destination was every bit as enjoyable as the reason we initially went.
