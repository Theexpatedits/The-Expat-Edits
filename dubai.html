DUBAI & RAS AL KHAIMAH

Dubai

Beyond the skyline.

OUR DUBAI

Two sides of the UAE.

Dubai has always been an exciting place to arrive. We began one of our trips at One&Only Royal Mirage, a lovely spot to settle into the city and enjoy a little of its more elegant side.

From there, we travelled north to Ras Al Khaimah and the desert at Al Wadi. It was a complete change of pace: a private-pool villa, a huge landscape to explore by bicycle, camels and wildlife along the way, and activities that kept the whole family busy.

We drove buggies across the dunes, flew birds of prey and spent an evening around a fire at a Bedouin gathering, eating and partying with the locals. Back at the hotel, an open-air cinema beneath the night sky made for another memorable evening.

It was a reminder that there is so much more to this part of the UAE than its extraordinary skyline.

WHERE WE STAYED

A city arrival, then a desert escape.

THE HOTELS

Two very different settings.

One&Only Royal Mirage was a lovely place to arrive in Dubai. Its gardens and surroundings offered a relaxed introduction to the city before we headed out towards Ras Al Khaimah.

The desert stay was a different experience altogether. At Al Wadi, the villa accommodation was superb, with our own private pool and the landscape right outside.

We also have a photograph from the Marriott that deserves a place in our Dubai story.

One&Only Royal Mirage

A lovely place to begin our time in Dubai.

Marriott

Another hotel detail from our time in Dubai.

THE DESERT VILLA

Our own little oasis.

One of the things we loved about the Al Wadi stay was the villa itself. Having a private pool gave us somewhere to relax between exploring the resort and heading out into the desert.

The accommodation was superb, and the setting made the whole experience feel very different from a conventional hotel stay. There was space, privacy and the desert all around us.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Out beyond the city.

THE LANDSCAPE

A different side of the UAE.

Leaving Dubai behind and heading north brought a complete change of scenery. The desert opened up around us, with its own pace, wildlife and sense of space.

DESERT ADVENTURE

Out on the dunes.

Driving buggies through the desert was great fun. It was a chance to get out beyond the resort and experience the landscape properly.

WILDLIFE

Life in the desert.

The resort was large enough to explore by bicycle, which became an adventure in itself. We saw camels and plenty of other wildlife along the way. There was always something worth stopping for.

BIRDS OF PREY

An experience the kids loved.

We had the opportunity to fly birds of prey during our stay. It was a memorable experience for the family and one of the activities that made the desert visit so distinctive.

THE BEDOUIN EVENING

Food, fire and good company.

One of our favourite memories was visiting a Bedouin gathering. We ate around a fire and spent the evening partying with the locals. It was a wonderful way to experience a different side of the region.

BACK IN THE CITY

The other side of Dubai.

UNDERWATER

A different kind of dive.

Dubai does not do things by halves. The aquarium experience at Atlantis was one of the more unusual things we did in the city, with the chance to dive among the marine life.

THE SKYLINE

Impossible to miss.

The scale of Dubai is part of its appeal. From the Burj Khalifa to the unmistakable Burj Al Arab, the city is full of landmarks that are instantly recognisable.

AFTER DARK

An open-air cinema beneath the stars.

Back at the hotel, the evenings had their own charm. We watched an open-air film beneath the night sky, a lovely way to finish a day spent exploring the desert.

Between the private villa, the wildlife, the activities and those quieter evenings, Al Wadi gave us a family experience that felt a world away from the city.

BEST TIME

The cooler months

The cooler months are a particularly good time to enjoy Dubai and spend time outdoors in the desert. The milder weather makes activities away from the city more comfortable.

THE CITY

Energy and ambition

Dubai brings together extraordinary hotels, restaurants, beaches and an ever-changing skyline. It can be as lively or as relaxed as you choose to make it.

BEYOND DUBAI

Ras Al Khaimah

Heading north opens up a different side of the UAE: desert landscapes, wildlife, outdoor adventures and a welcome sense of space.
