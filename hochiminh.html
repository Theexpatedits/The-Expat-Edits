OUR VIETNAM

Ho Chi Minh City

A weekend in the heart of Saigon.

OUR HO CHI MINH CITY

A city best discovered on foot.

We spent a weekend exploring Ho Chi Minh City, wandering through District 1, stopping for coffee, discovering little shops and finding somewhere to eat whenever something caught our attention.

From the historic architecture around the Central Post Office to the independent cafés inside The Cafe Apartments, the city offered something interesting at almost every turn.

Add a walk along the river, a bowl of local pho and the extraordinary energy of the streets, and it made for a wonderfully full weekend.

THE STAY

Fusion Suites Saigon.

A smart, comfortable base for a family weekend in the city.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A good room makes all the difference.

We stayed at Fusion Suites Saigon and were very happy with our choice. The hotel was smart and clean, and the room product was particularly good.

Travelling as a family, one of the things we appreciated most was the room arrangement. Having a separate room for the children worked really well and gave everyone a little more space.

The accommodation was well appointed and offered the sort of comfortable, practical base we wanted after spending the day exploring the city.

For a short city break, we thought it worked particularly well. We could head out into District 1, enjoy the cafés, shops and restaurants, and return somewhere comfortable at the end of the day.

OUR VERDICT

We'd happily recommend Fusion Suites for families looking for smart, comfortable accommodation with a useful separate sleeping arrangement for the children.

THE SETTING

District 1 is where we'd start.

Much of what we enjoyed about Ho Chi Minh City came from simply getting out and exploring. District 1 gave us plenty to discover, with shops, independent cafés, restaurants and historic landmarks all part of the mix.

We also really enjoyed walking along the river. It was a lovely change of pace from the busy streets and gave us another perspective on the city.

THE WAY TO EXPLORE

No complicated itinerary required.

Our favourite approach was to head out on foot, take our time and let the neighbourhood decide where we went next.

The best discoveries were often the places we hadn't planned to visit.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering Saigon.

A little history, independent cafés, colourful streets and some very good food.

THE CAFE APARTMENTS

A building full of little discoveries.

One of the places we particularly enjoyed exploring was The Cafe Apartments on Nguyen Hue Walking Street.

The building is filled with independent cafés, little shops and interesting spaces. It's exactly the sort of place we love wandering around, taking our time and seeing what we find.

THE OLD SAIGON

History around every corner.

We visited the Saigon Central Post Office, one of the city's most recognisable historic landmarks.

It was a lovely way to take in another side of the city, where the architecture and history provide a contrast to the pace of modern Saigon.

CAFE CULTURE

Time to slow down.

The cafés were another part of the experience we loved. We enjoyed stopping for a drink, taking in the surroundings and watching the city go by.

The independent café scene is a great reason to leave a little room in the day for unplanned stops.

THE LOCAL EXPERIENCE

A city best enjoyed at street level.

Some of our favourite moments were the simple ones: wandering through the streets, browsing the shops and finding somewhere to stop for something to eat.

Trying local pho was a real highlight. A bowl of Vietnamese noodle soup is a pretty wonderful way to experience the local food culture.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Making the most of a city break.

Ho Chi Minh City is a destination where a little structure goes a long way, but there's no need to plan every hour.

HOW LONG TO STAY

A weekend

We found a weekend was a lovely introduction to the city. Enough time to explore District 1, visit the main landmarks, enjoy the cafés and spend time eating our way around Saigon.

WHERE TO BASE YOURSELF

District 1

We'd suggest looking at accommodation in and around District 1. It provides a convenient starting point for shopping, restaurants, cafés and many of the central landmarks.

GETTING AROUND

Explore on foot

Walking was our favourite way to discover the city. Take your time, explore the side streets and stop whenever something catches your attention.

WHAT TO EAT

Start with pho

Trying local pho was one of our highlights. There are also plenty of opportunities to discover cafés, street food and local restaurants as you explore.

WHAT TO EXPLORE

Old and new Saigon

Combine the Central Post Office and historic city centre with the shops, cafés and independent spaces around Nguyen Hue.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Curious travellers

Couples and families who enjoy walking, eating, shopping, architecture and experiencing the everyday energy of a big Asian city.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Fusion Suites Saigon

Our hotel choice. Smart, clean and very well appointed, with a room arrangement that worked particularly well for our family.

02

The Cafe Apartments

A lovely building to explore on Nguyen Hue, filled with independent cafés, shops and interesting little spaces.

03

Saigon Central Post Office

A beautiful historic landmark and well worth including while exploring the centre.

04

Nguyen Hue

Spend some time around the pedestrian boulevard, particularly if you enjoy browsing cafés and shops.

05

Local Pho

One of our favourite food experiences. Make time to try a proper bowl of local Vietnamese pho.

06

The Saigon River

We really enjoyed walking along the river. A lovely change of pace from the busy streets of District 1.

OUR EDIT

A weekend best spent wandering.

Ho Chi Minh City was exactly the sort of destination we enjoy for a short break. We didn't need a packed itinerary or a long list of things to tick off. We simply headed out and explored.

District 1 became our natural starting point. We loved wandering around the shops and cafés, particularly The Cafe Apartments, and taking time to discover places along the way.

The historic Central Post Office gave us a glimpse of old Saigon, while walking along the river provided a welcome change of pace.

And then there was the food. Trying local pho was a highlight, as were the little stops for coffee and something to eat.

Fusion Suites worked really well for us as a family, giving us a comfortable base to return to after a day exploring.

Sometimes a weekend is all you need to get a feel for a city. Saigon certainly made us want to keep exploring.
