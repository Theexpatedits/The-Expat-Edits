The Experience

Hong Kong is a city of contrasts. One moment you're surrounded by towering glass skyscrapers, and the next you're wandering through quiet temples, bustling street markets or boarding a ferry to an island that feels worlds away. During our stay we discovered that the city is just as much about its atmosphere as its famous skyline.

From watching the iconic Star Ferry cross Victoria Harbour to exploring neighbourhoods where bamboo scaffolding still wraps modern buildings, Hong Kong constantly surprised us. Beyond the city centre we found beaches, hiking trails and peaceful islands that many visitors never experience, making it one of Asia's most rewarding destinations.

Our Journey

Three Moments We'll Never Forget

01

Setting Sail

Our adventure began as we left Labuan Bajo and sailed into Komodo National Park. Tiny islands emerged from impossibly blue water while secluded beaches and dramatic cliffs appeared around every corner. Travelling by boat gave us a completely different perspective of Indonesia and reminded us that the journey itself is often the highlight.

02

Face to Face with Dragons

Walking alongside the world's largest lizards was both exciting and humbling. Guided by experienced park rangers, we explored the rugged landscape while watching Komodo dragons in their natural habitat — a wildlife encounter unlike anything we've experienced elsewhere.

03

Beneath the Surface

The underwater world was every bit as memorable as the islands above. Swimming alongside sea turtles, colourful coral reefs and graceful manta rays made every snorkelling stop unforgettable, finishing our Komodo adventure in spectacular fashion.
