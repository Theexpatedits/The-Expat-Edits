The Experience

Where East Meets West

Hong Kong feels like our second home. We visit several times each year for both work and pleasure, and every trip reminds us why we love this city so much. The energy is infectious, the food scene is among the best in Asia, and there's always something new to discover.

One moment you're surrounded by towering skyscrapers and luxury shopping, the next you're wandering through quiet temples, bustling street markets or catching the Star Ferry across Victoria Harbour. Few cities blend old and new as effortlessly as Hong Kong.

Although we usually stay around Central and Wan Chai, some of our favourite memories have come from exploring beyond the city centre. Days spent in Repulse Bay, Shek O, Lamma Island and Lantau revealed a completely different side of Hong Kong that many visitors never experience.

For us, Hong Kong is far more than an incredible skyline. It's the atmosphere, the people, the food and the endless contrasts that keep bringing us back year after year.

Our Journey

Three Moments We'll Never Forget

01

Victoria Peak

Our first stop was Victoria Peak, and it immediately reminded us why Hong Kong has one of the world's most famous skylines. Looking across Victoria Harbour as the city came to life was an unforgettable way to begin the trip.

Watching ferries weave across the harbour while skyscrapers reflected the morning light was a moment we'll never forget. It remains one of the finest city views we've experienced anywhere in the world.

02

Between Tradition & Modernity

One of our favourite discoveries was seeing traditional bamboo scaffolding climbing the sides of modern skyscrapers. It's a reminder that Hong Kong has preserved many traditions despite being one of the world's most modern cities.

Every neighbourhood had its own personality, from busy street markets to peaceful temples tucked between glass towers. Walking the city became one of the highlights of the journey.

03

Beyond The Skyline

The biggest surprise was discovering how much nature surrounds Hong Kong. Within an hour we found ourselves on quiet beaches, scenic hiking trails and peaceful islands that felt a world away from the city.

That contrast is what makes Hong Kong so special. Few places allow you to experience one of the world's greatest skylines in the morning and relax beside the sea that same afternoon.
