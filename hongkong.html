The Experience

Hong Kong is like our second home, we travel here once a quarter for work and pleasure as we love the city’s vibe along with exciting restaurants and bar scene. Hong kong is a city of contrasts. One moment you're surrounded by towering glass skyscrapers, and the next you're wandering through quiet temples, bustling street markets or boarding a ferry to an island that feels worlds While we normally stay in Central or Wan Chai, we love getting around to other parts of the island and frequently find ourselves in Repulse Bay, Shek O, or having jumped on Star Ferry to head over to Kowloon side and even exploring islands like Lantau and Lamma

The city is just as much about its atmosphere as its famous skyline. From watching the iconic Star Ferry cross Victoria Harbour to exploring neighbourhoods where bamboo scaffolding still wraps modern buildings, Hong Kong constantly surprises us. Beyond the city centre we love the hiking, the beaches and culture that make it one of Asia's most rewarding destinations.

Our Journey

Three Moments We'll Never Forget

01

Victoria Peak

Our first stop was Victoria Peak, and it immediately set the tone for the rest of the trip. Looking out across Victoria Harbour, the skyline stretches in every direction, with towering skyscrapers framed by mountains and the South China Sea beyond. It is one of those rare viewpoints that genuinely lives up to its reputation.

We arrived early enough to enjoy the views before the crowds grew, watching ferries weave across the harbour while the city slowly came to life below. It was the perfect introduction to Hong Kong and one of the finest city panoramas we've experienced anywhere in the world.

02

Between Tradition and Modernity

One of the things we enjoyed most about Hong Kong was discovering the details hidden between the famous landmarks. Walking through the streets, we found bamboo scaffolding climbing the sides of modern buildings, a centuries-old construction technique still used throughout the city.

These small moments reminded us that despite its futuristic skyline, Hong Kong has held onto many traditions. Exploring on foot became one of the highlights of the trip, with every neighbourhood offering something completely different from the last.

03

Beyond The Skyline

Perhaps our biggest surprise was discovering just how much nature surrounds Hong Kong. A short journey from the busy streets led us to quiet beaches, green hills and peaceful islands that felt a world away from the city's energy.

This contrast is what makes Hong Kong so memorable. You can spend the morning among some of the tallest buildings on Earth and finish the afternoon beside the sea, making it one of the most diverse city destinations we've visited.
