The Experience

Asia's most exciting city

Hong Kong is one of those destinations that keeps drawing us back. Every visit feels different, whether we're wandering through the streets of Central, catching the Star Ferry across Victoria Harbour or escaping to one of the city's peaceful islands.

What makes Hong Kong so special is its incredible contrast. One moment you're surrounded by towering skyscrapers and world-class restaurants, the next you're walking quiet mountain trails, relaxing on golden beaches or discovering centuries-old temples hidden between modern buildings.

Although famous for its skyline, it's the atmosphere that stays with us most. The energy, efficiency, food and endless variety make Hong Kong one of our favourite destinations anywhere in the world.

01

Victoria Peak

Every visit begins at Victoria Peak. Watching Hong Kong spread out below us, with Victoria Harbour cutting through the centre of the city, is one of the world's great urban views. Whether you arrive during the day or just before sunset, it never disappoints.

02

The Streets Below

Back at street level, Hong Kong reveals a completely different personality. Traditional markets, tiny noodle shops and centuries-old temples sit comfortably alongside luxury hotels and some of Asia's finest restaurants, giving every neighbourhood its own character.

03

Beyond The Skyline

Some of our favourite memories have come from exploring beyond the city centre. From peaceful beaches and island ferries to mountain walks and coastal villages, Hong Kong offers far more than its famous skyline, which is why we find ourselves returning again and again.
