HONG KONG

Hong Kong

A second home of city energy, mountains and great nights.

The Journey

A city that feels like a second home.

Hong Kong has become something of a second home for us. I travel and work here regularly, which has given us the chance to explore far beyond the obvious parts of the city.

We love hiking Dragon's Back, getting out to Repulse Bay and finding new corners of the city whenever we're here. It's a place where the mountains, beaches and skyscrapers all sit within remarkably easy reach of one another.

And then there is the energy. The restaurants, the bars, the late nights and the feeling that there is always somewhere else to discover. Hong Kong is busy, social and occasionally chaotic — and that's exactly what we love about it.

01

The city

Hong Kong's skyline is part of what makes the place so addictive. Modern towers, older buildings, harbour views and busy streets all seem to compete for space.

Even after spending a lot of time here, there is always another street, view or building that catches your eye.

02

Beyond the towers

One of the things we love most about Hong Kong is how quickly the city disappears. Head into the hills and suddenly you're surrounded by greenery, sea and open space.

Dragon's Back is a favourite for exactly this reason — one of the best ways to see a completely different side of Hong Kong.

Hong Kong in motion

Beyond the City

Mountains & sea

Hong Kong is easy to think of as a city first, but some of our favourite days here are spent outside it.

Dragon's Back is a regular favourite, while Repulse Bay is an easy escape when we want to get away from the buildings and spend some time beside the water.

That contrast is one of the things that keeps us coming back. Few cities make it so easy to move between intense urban energy and open landscape.

After dark

The other side of Hong Kong

Eat & Drink

Come hungry, stay late.

The food and drinking scene is a big part of why we love Hong Kong. There is an enormous amount of choice, from serious restaurants to small bars and neighbourhood spots.

Carbone is a favourite for Italian, we're big fans of anything from the Black Sheep Restaurants group and Mott 32 is always worth a visit.

For drinks, we love finding the bars around Sun Street and Star Street. They're the sort of places that make an evening in Hong Kong feel spontaneous — somewhere for one drink that can easily turn into several.

The Atmosphere

Busy, social and full of energy.

Hong Kong is a city we like to experience rather than simply tick off. Walk the streets, find a restaurant, disappear into a bar and see where the evening takes you.

The combination of incredible food, great drinking, compact neighbourhoods and the constant movement of the city is what keeps bringing us back.

Our Take

A city that never really switches off.

Hong Kong has become a second home for us, and that's probably the best way to experience it. Don't try to see everything in one trip. Come back, explore a little further each time, find a new restaurant, walk another trail and stay out for another drink.
