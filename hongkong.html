OUR HONG KONG

Hong Kong

A city of contrasts. And one we never tire of exploring.

OUR HONG KONG

The city we always come back to.

Hong Kong is one of those cities that somehow feels familiar every time we arrive. Living in Singapore means it is only a short flight away, and between work trips and weekends away we've found ourselves returning again and again. Every visit reminds us why we love it.

What makes Hong Kong so special is the variety. One day can be spent wandering the streets of Central, discovering another restaurant or market. The next might involve hiking around The Peak, escaping to the beaches of the south side or spending an afternoon in Stanley.

We love the energy around Peel Street, the food, the shopping and the sheer character of the city. But we also love the quieter side of Hong Kong. It is a destination that offers far more than its famous skyline.

We've returned often enough to have our favourite places, but there always seems to be another street, restaurant or corner waiting to be discovered.

THE STAY

Two hotels we'd happily return to.

We don't have hotel photography from our stays in Hong Kong, but there are two properties we particularly like and would happily recommend when choosing somewhere to base yourself in the city.

OUR HOTEL EDIT

The Hari Hong Kong.

The Hari is a hotel we really enjoy staying at. It has a lovely atmosphere and offers a comfortable base for spending time in Hong Kong.

We particularly like having somewhere that feels welcoming to return to after a busy day exploring the city. Whether we're heading out for dinner, spending time in Central or simply enjoying another Hong Kong weekend, it's a property we are very happy to recommend.

A hotel we'd happily put on our shortlist whenever we're planning another visit.

LOCATION

The Pottinger Hong Kong.

The Pottinger is another hotel we really like. Its location is the big attraction for us.

Being so well positioned for Central makes it a particularly convenient choice when you want to get out and enjoy the city on foot. Soho, the restaurants, bars and the characterful streets of Central are all part of the appeal.

If exploring the city is high on your list, The Pottinger makes a very good base from which to do it.

Our choice when location and being in the middle of the action are priorities.

THE CITY

A city that never stands still.

Hong Kong is instantly recognisable, but the longer you spend here the more you realise how many different sides it has. The towers, the older neighbourhoods, the harbour and the green hills all sit remarkably close together.

THE SKYLINE

A city built upwards.

Hong Kong has a way of making scale feel almost impossible. Towers rise behind old streets, buildings seem to stretch endlessly upwards and the mountains appear almost wherever you look.

It's a city that constantly rewards looking up.

OLD & NEW

Where different worlds meet.

One of our favourite things about Hong Kong is the contrast between its old and new buildings. You can walk past a weathered shopfront and look up to see a contemporary skyscraper standing directly behind it.

These details give the city so much of its personality.

ABOVE THE CITY

The Peak. And a different side of Hong Kong.

We love exploring The Peak and hiking around the island. It's one of the things that makes Hong Kong so unusual. You can leave the busy streets behind and find yourself surrounded by greenery surprisingly quickly.

THE GREAT OUTDOORS

From the skyline to the trails.

The Peak is an obvious highlight, but for us the real enjoyment comes from getting out and exploring beyond the main viewpoint.

The walking routes around Victoria Peak offer wonderful views across the harbour, Kowloon and the surrounding islands. The combination of woodland paths and spectacular city views is hard to beat.

We love the feeling of being away from the city without actually having to leave it.

CENTRAL & SOHO

Peel Street and the pleasure of getting lost.

Some of our favourite Hong Kong experiences happen without much of a plan. We love spending time around Peel Street and wandering through the surrounding streets of Soho.

THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

Follow the streets and see where they lead.

Peel Street has a character all of its own. We enjoy the atmosphere, the restaurants and the feeling of being right in the middle of the city's everyday life.

It's a great area to spend time simply walking around, stopping for a coffee, finding somewhere for a drink or discovering another restaurant tucked into the hillside.

THE SOUTH SIDE

Repulse Bay, Stanley and a slower pace.

One of the things we love about Hong Kong is how quickly the atmosphere changes when you head towards the south side of the island. The towers and busy streets give way to coastline, beaches and a more relaxed pace.

REPULSE BAY

A little breathing space.

Repulse Bay is one of our favourite escapes from the intensity of Central. It's a lovely place to spend an afternoon, enjoy the beach and take in a completely different side of Hong Kong.

The setting is beautiful, with the bay framed by the surrounding hills and the South China Sea beyond.

It's a reminder that Hong Kong can be every bit as appealing when you slow down.

STANLEY

Markets, the waterfront and a village atmosphere.

We also really enjoy heading down to Stanley. It's a lovely contrast to the centre of Hong Kong and somewhere we can happily spend a good part of an afternoon.

Stanley Market is a great place to browse. We love strolling around the stalls, looking through the different shops and seeing what catches our eye.

Afterwards, the waterfront is the perfect place to slow things down. There are restaurants and cafes along Stanley Main Street, with lovely views across the bay.

FOOD & CITY LIFE

Good food, great streets and evenings out.

Food is a huge part of why we keep coming back to Hong Kong. Whether we're booking somewhere special or simply finding a neighbourhood restaurant, eating well is always part of the plan.

THE FOOD SCENE

A city that knows how to do dinner.

We never miss an excuse to book somewhere from the Black Sheep Group, especially Carbone. The New Punjab Club is another firm favourite.

There is always somewhere new to try, but it's just as enjoyable returning to the restaurants that have become part of our Hong Kong routine.

AFTER DARK

The city takes on another personality.

Hong Kong after dark is another reason we never feel we've quite finished exploring it.

The lights come on across the harbour, the restaurants fill up and the skyline takes on a completely different character.

It's a wonderful city to simply spend time in, without needing to be anywhere in particular.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Making the most of Hong Kong.

Hong Kong is a destination where a little flexibility goes a long way. You can combine city exploring, restaurants, shopping, beaches and hiking without travelling far. Here are a few things worth considering.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

October – April

We particularly enjoy the cooler months, when the weather is generally more comfortable for walking, exploring neighbourhoods and spending time outdoors.

Autumn and spring are especially appealing for combining city visits with hiking.

WEATHER & CONDITIONS

Subtropical

Hong Kong has hot, humid summers and generally milder winters. Summer can be particularly warm and wet, with occasional tropical storms.

If hiking is part of your plans, the cooler months are usually more comfortable.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Four to five days

We'd suggest allowing at least four or five days to enjoy Hong Kong properly. That gives you time for Central, the Peak, the harbour, restaurants and an afternoon or two on the south side.

GETTING AROUND

Walk, MTR & ferry

Hong Kong is remarkably easy to navigate. The MTR is efficient, taxis are convenient and the ferries are a wonderful way to experience the harbour.

We also find that walking is one of the best ways to discover the city.

THE GREAT OUTDOORS

More than a city break

The Peak offers some wonderful walking routes. The Peak Circle Walk is a particularly enjoyable option, while the wider island has plenty of hiking opportunities.

Allow time for Repulse Bay and Stanley if you want to explore the south side.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Almost everyone

Hong Kong works brilliantly for couples, families, food lovers, shoppers and anyone who enjoys a mixture of city life and outdoor adventure.

There is plenty to do without feeling that you need to follow a packed itinerary.

OUR EDIT

A city best enjoyed in different ways.

For us, Hong Kong is about balance. We love the energy of Central, the restaurants, the shopping and the feeling of being surrounded by the city.

But we also love heading up to The Peak, exploring the walking trails and getting out into the greenery. Repulse Bay and Stanley offer another side of Hong Kong entirely, and we always enjoy spending time down there.

We'd suggest not trying to do everything in one visit. Pick a neighbourhood, explore it properly, stop for lunch and let the day develop.

Hong Kong has so many different personalities. That's exactly why we keep coming back.
