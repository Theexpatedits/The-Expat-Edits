The Experience

More than just a skyline

We travel to Hong Kong several times a year for both work and pleasure, and every visit reminds us why we love it so much. The city's incredible energy, world-class restaurants and vibrant bar scene keep drawing us back.

Hong Kong is a city of contrasts. One moment you're surrounded by soaring skyscrapers, the next you're wandering through quiet temples, bustling street markets or boarding a ferry to an island that feels a world away.

While we normally stay around Central and Wan Chai, some of our favourite days are spent exploring further afield. We love escaping to Repulse Bay, Shek O, Lantau Island and Lamma Island, while a ride across Victoria Harbour on the Star Ferry never gets old.

Beyond its famous skyline, it's the atmosphere that keeps bringing us back. Bamboo scaffolding still wraps modern buildings, every neighbourhood has its own personality, and some of Asia's best food can be found around almost every corner. For us, Hong Kong remains one of the most exciting cities in the world.

Our Journey

Three Moments We'll Never Forget

01

Victoria Peak

Our first stop was Victoria Peak, and it immediately set the tone for the rest of the trip. Looking out across Victoria Harbour, the skyline stretches in every direction, with towering skyscrapers framed by mountains and the South China Sea beyond.

We arrived early enough to enjoy the views before the crowds grew, watching ferries weave across the harbour while the city slowly came to life below. It was the perfect introduction to Hong Kong and one of the finest city panoramas we've experienced anywhere in the world.

02

Between Tradition and Modernity

One of the things we enjoyed most about Hong Kong was discovering the details hidden between the famous landmarks. Walking through the streets, we found bamboo scaffolding climbing the sides of modern buildings, a centuries-old construction technique still used throughout the city.

These small moments reminded us that despite its futuristic skyline, Hong Kong has held onto many traditions. Exploring on foot became one of the highlights of the trip, with every neighbourhood offering something completely different from the last.

03

Beyond The Skyline

Perhaps our biggest surprise was discovering just how much nature surrounds Hong Kong. A short journey from the busy streets led us to quiet beaches, green hills and peaceful islands that felt a world away from the city's energy.

This contrast is what makes Hong Kong so memorable. You can spend the morning among some of the tallest buildings on Earth and finish the afternoon beside the sea, making it one of the most diverse city destinations we've visited.
