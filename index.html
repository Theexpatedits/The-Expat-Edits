Welcome to The Expat Edits.

We’re a family of four making the most of living in this corner of the world. We’re not professional photographers — we simply enjoy documenting our travels as we explore Asia.

Through our Instagram and the editions on this site, we hope to share the places we discover, along with tips, recommendations and a little inspiration for anyone lucky enough to be travelling through the region.

Enjoy flicking through some of the images, places and ideas we’ve picked up along the way.

OUR HOME

Singapore

Life, travel & everything in between

Singapore is home for us now. It’s where we live, work and spend most of our time, but it’s also the starting point for so much of the travelling we do around Asia.

The Expat Edits grew out of that life — the places we visit, the hotels we stay in, the photographs we take and the things we notice along the way.
