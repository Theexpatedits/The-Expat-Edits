JAPAN

Japan

Tokyo, Mount Fuji and a lot of walking.

The Journey

Tokyo, explored on foot.

Japan was a brilliant trip for us, and Tokyo was a city we really enjoyed getting under the skin of. We spent hours walking, exploring different districts and parks, with plenty of stops along the way for food, drinks and shopping.

One of the things we particularly enjoyed was discovering the network of bars and restaurants tucked underneath Tokyo Station. It's the sort of detail that makes Tokyo so much fun — there always seems to be another street, neighbourhood or little place waiting to be found.

We also headed out of the city to see Mount Fuji, staying on Lake Yamanakako. The open-air onsen, tucked away among the woodland, was a highlight and a very different experience from the energy of Tokyo.

01

Start walking

Tokyo is one of those cities that rewards simply heading out and seeing where the day takes you. We walked for miles, moving between neighbourhoods and discovering something different around almost every corner.

The parks, streets, architecture and constant movement make the city incredibly easy to explore on foot.

02

Tokyo after dark

Some of our favourite discoveries were underneath Tokyo Station, where bars and restaurants fill the spaces beneath the city.

It's a great place to wander without much of a plan, stopping wherever looks interesting and seeing where the evening takes you.

From the camera

Tokyo

Exploring Tokyo

Parks, districts & shopping

We spent a lot of time simply exploring. Tokyo is enormous, but each district has its own character and walking between them gives you a much better sense of the city.

The parks provided a welcome change of pace, while the shopping was some of the best we've experienced. There was always another store, street or small neighbourhood to discover.

Tokyo

Keep exploring

Beyond Tokyo

Mount Fuji

We eventually escaped the city and headed out to see Mount Fuji, staying on the shores of Lake Yamanakako.

The change of pace was exactly what we needed. After the intensity of Tokyo, the lake, mountains and woodland felt incredibly peaceful.

Lake Yamanakako

A different Japan

Where We Stayed

Lake Yamanakako

We stayed at a Marriott on Lake Yamanakako and it was a completely different experience from Tokyo. The woodland setting was peaceful and the location gave us the chance to slow down for a few days.

The open-air onsen surrounded by woodland was the highlight. Sitting outside amongst the trees after the intensity of Tokyo was one of our favourite moments of the trip.

Our Take

Tokyo energy, Fuji calm.

Japan worked brilliantly for us because it gave us two very different experiences in one trip. Tokyo was all about walking, eating, shopping and discovering new neighbourhoods, while Lake Yamanakako gave us the space to slow down and enjoy the landscape.
