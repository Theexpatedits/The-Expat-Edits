OUR JAPAN

Japan

Tokyo, Fuji-San & the joy of discovering somewhere new.

OUR JAPAN

One extraordinary city. A mountain escape. Two very different sides of Japan.

We loved Japan. Tokyo was everything we hoped it would be and more: a city made for walking, discovering and constantly finding something interesting just around the next corner.

We spent our days exploring neighbourhoods, parks and temples, eating sushi at the morning market, discovering little izakayas beneath the railway tracks and shopping along Cat Street.

Then we headed out to Lake Yamanaka. The pace changed completely. Woodland, an outdoor onsen and wonderful views of Mount Fuji made it the perfect contrast to the energy of Tokyo.

WHERE WE STAYED · TOKYO

Park Hyatt Tokyo.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A hotel we loved returning to.

We also stayed at the Park Hyatt Tokyo and thoroughly enjoyed our time there.

We don't have photographs from the hotel to include in this Edition, but it deserves a mention. It was one of the places that helped make our Tokyo stay so enjoyable.

If you're looking for a hotel in Tokyo, it is certainly one we'd be happy to recommend based on our own experience.

WHERE WE STAYED · BEYOND TOKYO

Marriott Lake Yamanaka.

After the energy of Tokyo, heading out towards Lake Yamanaka was exactly the change of pace we wanted. Woodland, fresh air and the chance to see Fuji-San made this a very different experience of Japan.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A slower side of Japan.

We stayed at the Marriott near Lake Yamanaka, surrounded by woodland. After spending days walking around Tokyo, it was lovely to slow down and enjoy a completely different setting.

One of the highlights was using the outdoor onsen in the woods. Sitting in the hot water, surrounded by trees, was one of those simple travel experiences that stays with you.

We also had some wonderful views of Mount Fuji. We were lucky with the weather during our stay, with the clouds clearing and the mountain appearing in all its glory.

It was a memorable contrast to the city and somewhere we were very glad we had included in our trip.

THE SETTING

Woodland, water and Fuji-San.

The surrounding woodland and the opportunity to enjoy the outdoor onsen made this stay particularly special.

It was a lovely place to step away from the pace of Tokyo.

THE CITY

Tokyo is best discovered on foot.

We covered a serious number of miles in Tokyo, and that was exactly how we wanted to experience it. Rather than rushing between a list of attractions, we enjoyed wandering through the city and seeing where the day took us.

THE STREETS

A city that keeps surprising you.

Tokyo has an incredible energy. Busy intersections, narrow streets, traditional buildings and modern architecture all sit alongside one another.

We loved simply walking, looking around and letting the city reveal itself. Some of our favourite discoveries were the ones we hadn't planned.

THE MORNING MARKET

Sushi for breakfast. And a cold beer.

One of our favourite Tokyo memories was breakfast at the morning fish market. Incredible sushi, fresh seafood and a cold beer to go with it.

It was a pretty perfect way to start the day, and one of those experiences that makes travelling in Japan so enjoyable.

FOOD & DRINK

Under the railway tracks.

We particularly loved the atmosphere around Tokyo's railway lines. Small izakayas, lanterns and restaurants tucked beneath the tracks created a completely different side of the city.

There was something wonderfully informal about dipping in and out of these places and seeing what was on offer.

PARKS & TEMPLES

A quieter side of Tokyo.

One of the things we loved most was how quickly the atmosphere could change. A busy street would lead into a park, a shrine or a beautiful temple.

We enjoyed finding these quieter corners just as much as the more obvious sights.

DISCOVERY

Traditional details and modern Tokyo.

The contrast between old and new was everywhere. We loved the traditional Japanese details, the temples and the little discoveries along the way.

THE GREEN SPACES

Finding room to slow down.

Tokyo's parks were another highlight. After walking through the busy neighbourhoods, it was lovely to find green spaces and beautifully maintained gardens.

These little changes of pace were a big part of what made exploring the city so enjoyable.

MORE TO DISCOVER

Shopping, temples and the unexpected.

Tokyo is a city of contrasts. We loved moving between its traditional corners and the more contemporary neighbourhoods, finding something different around every turn.

CAT STREET

Shopping and people-watching.

Cat Street was one of our favourite places to spend time. Great for clothes shopping, wandering and people-watching, with plenty to discover along the way.

THE TEMPLES

Never far from something beautiful.

We loved finding temples and shrines during our walks. They provided a completely different atmosphere from the busy streets around them.

THE EXPERIENCE

Fuji-San and a memorable breakfast.

Sometimes the simplest moments become the best memories. For us, seeing Mount Fuji and stopping for pancakes at The Park Cafe was one of those moments.

THE PARK CAFE

Pancakes with a view.

We were incredibly lucky with the weather. The clouds parted while we were there and suddenly Mount Fuji was completely visible.

We celebrated with pancakes for breakfast at The Park Cafe. Nothing particularly complicated, but it was one of those moments that made the trip memorable.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Two destinations. One wonderful trip.

We loved combining Tokyo with a few days around Lake Yamanaka. The contrast between the city and the countryside was one of the things that made the trip work so well.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

Spring & autumn

Both are wonderful periods to visit Japan. Spring brings blossom and milder weather, while autumn offers beautiful colours and comfortable conditions for walking.

GETTING AROUND TOKYO

Walk & train

We walked huge distances around Tokyo and used the train network when we needed to cover more ground. The combination worked brilliantly.

BEYOND TOKYO

Lake Yamanaka

A wonderful change of pace, with woodland, Mount Fuji and the chance to enjoy an outdoor onsen.

GETTING THERE

By train

We travelled by train from Tokyo towards the Lake Yamanaka area and back again. We loved the journey and would happily recommend including it in the trip.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Allow time for both

We'd recommend giving Tokyo enough time to explore its different neighbourhoods before heading out to the countryside for a slower few days.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

City & countryside

Japan works brilliantly for families, couples and anyone who enjoys food, culture, walking, shopping and a little adventure.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Park Hyatt Tokyo

A hotel we stayed at and thoroughly enjoyed. We don't have photographs from the property, but it deserves a place in our Tokyo recommendations.

02

Marriott Lake Yamanaka

Our choice for a slower stay outside Tokyo. Woodland surroundings, an outdoor onsen and the opportunity to enjoy views of Mount Fuji.

03

Tsukiji Fish Market

Go hungry. Sushi for breakfast and a cold beer made this one of our favourite Tokyo memories.

04

Tokyo's izakayas

We loved the little bars and restaurants, particularly those tucked beneath the railway tracks. A brilliant way to experience another side of the city.

05

Cat Street

Great for clothes shopping, wandering and people-watching. One of the areas we particularly enjoyed.

06

Senso-ji and Tokyo's temples

We loved finding temples and shrines as we explored the city. They offered a wonderful contrast to the busy streets.

07

Roppongi at night

Walking the streets, dipping in and out of bars and seeing where the evening took us was one of the most enjoyable spontaneous parts of our trip.

08

The Park Cafe

Pancakes for breakfast and Mount Fuji appearing through the clouds. A simple experience that became a particularly lovely memory.

09

Tokyo on foot

Our biggest recommendation. Pick a direction and walk. Some of the best parts of Tokyo are what you discover between the places you planned to visit.

OUR EDIT

Experience the energy. Then find the quiet.

Japan was one of those trips where the contrast between destinations made the whole experience even better.

We loved Tokyo. The walking, the food, the little izakayas, the markets and the constant discoveries. It is a city that rewards curiosity and one we could happily spend more time exploring.

But heading out towards Lake Yamanaka was just as memorable. The train journey, the woodland, the outdoor onsen and the views of Fuji-San gave us a completely different experience of Japan.

And sometimes it is the simplest things that stay with you. Sushi and a cold beer for breakfast, or pancakes at The Park Cafe with Mount Fuji in the background.

If we were planning the trip again, we'd absolutely include both.
