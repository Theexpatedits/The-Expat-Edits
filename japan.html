Japan

From the energy of Tokyo to ancient temples, autumn colours and the timeless beauty of Mount Fuji, Japan is a journey that stays with you long after you leave.

The Experience

More than just Tokyo

Japan is one of those places where almost everything feels familiar and completely different at the same time. The energy of Tokyo is extraordinary, but what makes the country so rewarding is how quickly that intensity gives way to quiet temples, beautiful gardens and landscapes that feel almost impossibly peaceful.

We loved the contrast. One moment you're surrounded by neon, crowds and the constant movement of the city, and the next you're standing beneath ancient wooden gates or looking across a landscape of autumn colours towards Mount Fuji.

What stayed with us most was the attention to detail. From the food and hospitality to the architecture, gardens and simple everyday rituals, Japan has a remarkable ability to make even ordinary moments feel special. It's a destination that rewards slowing down and paying attention.

01

Tokyo After Dark

Tokyo after dark is something you really have to experience to understand. Neon streets, endless movement and crowds that somehow seem to flow around one another create an energy unlike anywhere else. It's busy, chaotic and completely captivating.

02

Tradition in the City

One of the things we loved most about Japan was the contrast between the modern and the traditional. Beautiful temples, wooden gates and quiet gardens can appear almost unexpectedly among the energy of the cities, offering a completely different pace and atmosphere.

03

Beyond Tokyo

Japan becomes even more special when you get beyond the city. Autumn colours, peaceful landscapes and views towards Mount Fuji provide a wonderful change of pace. This quieter side of Japan was every bit as memorable as the excitement of Tokyo.

OUR FAVOURITE

Tokyo

Tokyo is the perfect place to begin a Japan journey. Staying somewhere central puts you close to the energy of the city while making it easy to explore its quieter neighbourhoods, temples, restaurants and bars. We loved having the city on our doorstep and being able to head out and explore without needing a plan.

ALSO RECOMMENDED

Beyond Tokyo

If you have the time, spending a few nights outside Tokyo completely changes the rhythm of the trip. Being closer to the mountains, temples and countryside gives you the opportunity to slow down and experience the quieter side of Japan. It is also where some of the country's most beautiful seasonal scenery can be found.
