The Experience

Where tradition meets tomorrow

Japan had been high on our bucket list for years, and somehow it still managed to exceed every expectation. Tokyo is unlike anywhere else we've visited. It's fast-paced and energetic, yet somehow remains incredibly clean, organised and welcoming. Every neighbourhood feels like discovering a completely different city.

Some of our favourite moments came from simply wandering without a plan. One minute we'd be surrounded by neon lights, designer stores and busy crossings, the next we'd stumble across a peaceful shrine, a tiny family-run restaurant or a quiet backstreet that felt completely untouched by time.

Leaving Tokyo for Mount Fuji was the perfect contrast. Trading skyscrapers for open landscapes and waking each morning to Japan's most iconic mountain reminded us just how diverse this country really is. Experiencing both sides of Japan made this one of our favourite trips in Asia.

01

The Heart of Tokyo

Tokyo is one of those cities that somehow lives up to the hype. From the organised chaos of Shibuya Crossing to peaceful gardens tucked between skyscrapers, every day brought something completely unexpected. It's a city that rewards curiosity.

02

Old Traditions

Despite being one of the world's most modern countries, Japan never loses sight of its heritage. Ancient temples, beautiful shrines and centuries-old customs sit comfortably alongside futuristic architecture, giving the country a character unlike anywhere else.

03

Mount Fuji

Our stay at the Fuji Marriott offered a completely different side of Japan. With Mount Fuji dominating the skyline, the pace slowed, the scenery became breathtaking and we were reminded why this mountain has become one of the country's greatest symbols.

OUR FAVOURITE

Fuji Marriott Hotel Lake Yamanaka

Nestled amongst forests with spectacular views towards Mount Fuji, the Fuji Marriott combines modern comfort with peaceful surroundings. It's the perfect place to slow down after the energy of Tokyo and makes an excellent base for exploring the Fuji Five Lakes region.

ALSO RECOMMENDED

Aman Tokyo

For anyone looking to experience Tokyo at its most luxurious, Aman Tokyo delivers exceptional service, elegant Japanese design and one of the finest hotel experiences anywhere in the city.
