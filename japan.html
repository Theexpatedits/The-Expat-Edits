JAPAN

Japan

Tokyo, Mount Fuji and a lot of walking.

THE JOURNEY

Tokyo is best discovered on foot.

We loved Tokyo. More than anything, we loved walking it. We covered a serious number of miles, moving between neighbourhoods, parks and temples and constantly finding something that made us want to stop and look.

Some of our favourite moments were the simplest ones — finding somewhere to eat in the tunnels beneath Tokyo Station, wandering through the streets around Cat Street or just heading into another park to escape the pace of the city for a while.

Then we headed out towards Mount Fuji and stayed at Lake Yamanaka. The change of pace was fantastic: woodland, the mountain right in front of us and an outdoor onsen where we could sit in the hot water surrounded by trees.

TOKYO

Walk until you get lost

THE CITY

The best bits are often the unexpected ones.

Tokyo has all the big-ticket sights, but we found ourselves enjoying the smaller discoveries just as much. The tunnels beneath Tokyo Station were a particular favourite — somewhere to wander, choose a place that looked good and eat.

We also loved Cat Street for shopping, while the city's parks and temples gave us plenty of chances to slow down between the busier parts of the trip.

BEYOND TOKYO

Up close with Mount Fuji.

We stayed at the Marriott at Lake Yamanaka, which gave us the chance to get properly close to Mount Fuji rather than simply seeing it in the distance from Tokyo.

It was a completely different side of Japan. After the energy and scale of Tokyo, being beside the lake with the mountain dominating the landscape felt incredibly peaceful.

LAKE YAMANAKA

Fuji country

OUR EDIT

Slow down at Lake Yamanaka.

The Marriott at Lake Yamanaka was a really good contrast to Tokyo. The setting among the woodland made it feel peaceful, while Mount Fuji was close enough to feel like part of the experience rather than simply something we'd gone to photograph.

The outdoor onsen was a particular highlight. Sitting in the hot water outside, surrounded by forest, was exactly what we wanted after spending days walking around Tokyo.

We also really enjoyed the journey back to Tokyo by train. After the quiet of the lake, watching the landscape change as we headed back into the city was a great way to finish that part of the trip.

OUR EDIT

Walk Tokyo. Then find the quiet.

Japan worked brilliantly for us because the two halves of the trip felt so different. Tokyo gave us endless streets to explore, food to discover, shopping, parks and temples, while Lake Yamanaka brought us right up against Mount Fuji and gave us somewhere peaceful to slow down.
