INDONESIA

Java

Ancient temples, coffee country & the quieter side of Indonesia.

OUR JAVA

A different side of Indonesia.

Java was a journey of contrasts. We began at Borobudur, one of Indonesia's most extraordinary historic sites, arriving before sunrise to watch the temple emerge as the light changed across the surrounding landscape.

From there, we moved away from the crowds and into the greener countryside, where villages, plantations and volcanic mountains gradually became the defining features of the trip.

Our stays at Le Temple and MesaStila gave us two very different experiences. We also enjoyed a special lunch at Amanjiwo, which deserves a mention in its own right.

What we remember most, however, is the pace of Java. It is a destination that rewards taking your time. Long lunches, walks through coffee plantations, quiet mornings and simply enjoying the scenery became some of our favourite moments.

THE JOURNEY

Where the story begins.

01 / BOROBUDUR

Before the world wakes up.

Borobudur was the natural starting point for our Java journey. We arrived before sunrise, when the atmosphere was still quiet and the surrounding landscape was gradually coming into view.

Watching the first light settle over the temple was one of those travel experiences that makes an early start entirely worthwhile.

The architecture, the stonework and the enormous scale of the monument are impressive enough during the day. But seeing it at first light gives the experience a completely different feeling.

THE EXPERIENCE

A place worth taking your time over.

One of the things we enjoyed about Borobudur was the opportunity to explore rather than simply arrive, take a photograph and leave.

There is so much detail in the architecture, from the individual stone reliefs to the distinctive stupas that make the temple instantly recognisable.

We would absolutely recommend planning an early visit and allowing enough time to appreciate the wider setting as well as the monument itself.

LOOKING BACK

The temple never really leaves the view.

Even after leaving Borobudur, the temple continued to appear in the distance as we travelled through the surrounding countryside.

It is a remarkable setting, and the relationship between the temple and the landscape is a big part of what makes this part of Java so special.

02 / BEYOND THE TEMPLE

The journey becomes the destination.

ON THE ROAD

A greener, quieter Java.

Leaving Borobudur, we headed north towards the mountains and the countryside around MesaStila.

This was where Java began to feel very different. The scenery became increasingly green, with villages, agricultural land and mountain views appearing along the way.

Having a private driver made the journey between our different stops straightforward. We could enjoy the scenery and take our time rather than worrying about navigating the roads ourselves.

THE LANDSCAPE

Mountains, plantations & village life.

Java is an island of enormous scale and variety. The landscape changes constantly, with fertile farmland, tropical vegetation and volcanic mountains providing a beautiful backdrop.

We particularly enjoyed the quieter moments away from the main attractions. Seeing how the countryside changes as you travel through it is part of the appeal.

A DIFFERENT PACE

Sometimes the best moments are the simplest.

Not every part of a trip needs to be an excursion. Some of our favourite memories came from simply stopping, enjoying the scenery and taking in the atmosphere.

Java is particularly good for that. It has a wonderful ability to slow things down.

03 / WHERE WE STAYED

Le Temple.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A convenient base for Borobudur.

We stayed at Le Temple during our time around Borobudur. Its location worked well for visiting the temple, particularly with an early start on the itinerary.

The room configuration also worked well for us as a family. Having the right amount of space and a practical arrangement makes a difference when travelling with children.

There were a few things we felt could be better, particularly the pool area, where the seating wasn't especially practical for spending time relaxing together.

Overall, it served its purpose, but having experienced some of the other properties nearby, we would make a different choice if returning.

OUR ALTERNATIVE

Plataran.

We enjoyed a couple of lunches at Plataran and really liked the setting. Having experienced the property, it is where we would look to stay next time we visit the Borobudur area.

If you are planning a visit and choosing a hotel around the temple, Plataran would be our first recommendation to investigate.

04 / OUR STAY

MesaStila.

A MEMORABLE STAY

A little more room to settle in.

MesaStila was one of the highlights of our Java trip, and we were fortunate enough to receive a wonderful upgrade.

We were moved into the former owner's house, which was an absolutely enormous residence. It was a very special place to spend time and a rather unexpected upgrade that made the stay particularly memorable.

The property itself has a very different atmosphere from the Borobudur area. Surrounded by mountains, plantations and greenery, it is somewhere you can genuinely slow down.

THE PROPERTY

Space, character & a sense of history.

The upgrade to the former owner's house was a real highlight. Its enormous proportions and character made it feel very different from a conventional hotel room.

It also gave us the opportunity to enjoy the property at a much more relaxed pace.

ONE OF OUR FAVOURITE EXPERIENCES

Into the coffee plantation.

One of the things we particularly enjoyed at MesaStila was exploring the coffee plantation. It is a lovely way to spend time outdoors and gives you a much better appreciation of the agricultural landscape surrounding the resort.

We also enjoyed exploring the vegetable gardens and the surrounding countryside. It was a completely different experience from our earlier days around Borobudur.

WELLNESS

Make time for the hammam.

Another experience we really enjoyed was the hammam at MesaStila.

After exploring the plantation and spending time outdoors, it was a lovely way to unwind. It is one of those experiences that adds something beyond simply having a comfortable hotel room.

Between the hammam, the walks and the peaceful setting, MesaStila was a property where we could happily have spent longer.

05 / A SPECIAL EXPERIENCE

Amanjiwo.

WORTH THE SPLURGE

A memorable lunch.

We didn't stay at Amanjiwo, but we did enjoy a particularly good lunch there.

It is undoubtedly a special property and one of those places where the setting is just as important as the experience itself.

It is certainly a splurge, but if your budget stretches that far, we would recommend making the effort to visit.

Even if you aren't planning to stay, experiencing the property over lunch is a lovely way to see what makes it so distinctive.

OUR THOUGHTS

Sometimes a special lunch is enough.

We always enjoy discovering hotels and restaurants that offer something a little different. Amanjiwo was one of those experiences, and it was a lovely addition to our time around Borobudur.

06 / THE LANDSCAPE

A land of volcanoes.

FROM THE GROUND

Mountains at every turn.

One of the defining features of Java is its volcanic landscape. The mountains appear throughout the journey, sometimes in the distance and sometimes dominating the horizon.

The combination of fertile land, tropical vegetation and dramatic mountain scenery gives the island a character all of its own.

THE JOURNEY HOME

One last look from above.

Having spent much of the trip surrounded by mountains and volcanoes, it was lovely to see the landscape again from the air.

The scale of the island becomes even more apparent from above, with the volcanic peaks rising out of the surrounding land.

It was a fitting end to a journey that had taken us from ancient temples into some of the quieter and greener corners of Indonesia.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

A few things worth knowing.

BEST TIME

May – October

The drier season is generally the best time to explore Java, particularly if you are planning temple visits, walking and time in the mountains.

GETTING AROUND

Private driver

We found having a driver particularly useful when travelling between Borobudur and the surrounding countryside. It makes the journey much more relaxed.

HOW LONG

Don't rush it

Allow time to enjoy both the temple area and the countryside. The journey between the different locations is part of the experience.

OUR TAKE

Java rewards slowing down.

Java wasn't about ticking off as many attractions as possible. It was about experiencing a different side of Indonesia.

Borobudur gave us the history and the unforgettable early mornings. The journey north introduced us to a greener, quieter landscape, while MesaStila gave us the opportunity to slow down and enjoy somewhere truly special.

Add in a memorable lunch at Amanjiwo, the coffee plantation, the hammam and the extraordinary volcanic scenery, and we came away with a real appreciation for this part of the island.

Our advice is simple: don't rush Java. Allow time to explore, take the long lunch and enjoy the journey.
