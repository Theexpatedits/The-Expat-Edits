OUR KOH LANTA

Koh Lanta

Slow days, warm water & island life.

OUR KOH LANTA

A beautiful place to stay. An island that reminded us to slow down.

Koh Lanta was exactly the sort of family escape we were looking for. We stayed at Pimalai Resort & Spa in a wonderful three-storey private pool villa, with enough space for everyone to enjoy their own little corner of the holiday.

The villa quickly became the centre of everything. There were mornings spent at Muay Thai, afternoons around the pool, long walks along the beach and evenings enjoying fresh seafood cooked for us at home.

We weren't looking for a packed itinerary. Koh Lanta gave us somewhere beautiful to settle in, spend time together and enjoy doing very little.

THE STAY

Pimalai Resort & Spa.

An exceptional family villa, beautiful views across the Andaman Sea and the sort of space that makes it very easy to stay a little longer.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A villa with room for everyone.

Pimalai offered us a fantastic villa spread over three levels. It was an outstanding room product and, importantly for a family holiday, gave everyone plenty of space.

The children had an entire floor to themselves, which meant they could enjoy their own space while we had somewhere to retreat to as well. It made a real difference to the experience.

At the top of the villa was a private swimming pool with amazing views across the sea. On the other side of the pool was a separate building, providing an independent lounge area and kitchen.

It felt more like having our own private residence than simply booking a hotel room. The layout, the views and the amount of space made this a particularly special place to stay.

For families looking for something more spacious than a conventional hotel suite, this villa configuration is well worth considering.

THE SETTING

Sea views, tropical gardens and somewhere to unwind.

One of the lovely things about Pimalai was how naturally the resort blended into its surroundings. Tropical greenery, beautiful views and the sea always seemed to be part of the picture.

The pool was a particularly lovely place to spend time, especially as the light changed towards the end of the day.

LIFE AT THE VILLA

Good food, family time and nowhere else to be.

Some of our favourite moments happened without leaving the villa.

DINING AT HOME

A private barbecue with fresh seafood.

One evening we arranged a barbecue at the villa. The chefs arrived with fresh seafood and cooked for us, turning dinner into a lovely experience without having to go anywhere.

It was exactly the sort of luxury that works particularly well when travelling as a family. Everyone could relax, enjoy the food and make the most of the villa.

With the open air, the views and our own private space, it was a memorable evening and one of the highlights of the stay.

The best part was not having to think about getting everyone ready to go out afterwards.

BEYOND THE VILLA

Discovering Koh Lanta.

THE MORNING ROUTINE

A little Muay Thai before the day began.

One of my favourite parts of the holiday was getting up each morning with my son to enjoy Muay Thai classes.

It gave us a reason to get moving, a little structure to the mornings and some time together before the rest of the day slowed down.

It was a lovely way to balance a relaxed family break with something active.

THE BEACH

Long walks and plenty of space.

The beach was vast, which was one of the things we particularly enjoyed about Koh Lanta.

We had some lovely walks along the sand, with plenty of room to enjoy the coastline without feeling that we needed to be anywhere in particular.

It was a simple pleasure, but one that became part of the rhythm of the holiday.

THE EVENINGS

Sunsets and the slower side of island life.

The evenings were often as memorable as the days. Watching the light change across the sea and enjoying the colours of the sunset became part of the experience.

There was no need to fill every evening with plans. Sometimes the view was enough.

THE UNEXPECTED MOMENTS

A few surprises along the way.

ISLAND LIFE

Even the monkeys wanted to join us.

Of course, no island holiday is complete without a little unexpected entertainment.

One evening we were robbed by monkeys! It was certainly an unexpected part of the experience and gave us a story to take home.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

When to go and how to make the most of it.

Koh Lanta is a destination where the right accommodation and a little flexibility can make a real difference. Here are a few things worth considering before you book.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

November – April

The dry season is generally the best time to enjoy Koh Lanta, with sunnier days and calmer seas.

The wetter months can bring heavier rain and more unsettled sea conditions.

THE EXPERIENCE

Slow and relaxed

Long beaches, warm water, tropical scenery and a wonderfully unhurried island rhythm.

It is particularly appealing if you are looking for a holiday where you can enjoy your accommodation rather than constantly travelling around.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Five to seven days

We'd suggest allowing enough time to enjoy the resort properly, explore the island if you wish and settle into a slower pace.

A longer stay works particularly well when you have a villa with plenty of space for the family.

GETTING THERE

Thailand's Andaman coast

Koh Lanta is reached via the southern Thailand travel network, with onward road and ferry connections depending on your arrival point.

Allow some time for the final transfer when planning your journey.

GETTING AROUND

Take it at your own pace

There is more to discover around the island, but one of the attractions of Koh Lanta is that you don't need to be constantly on the move.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Families and couples

Koh Lanta works particularly well for those who enjoy comfortable accommodation, beautiful beaches and a slower holiday.

A spacious private pool villa makes it especially appealing for families.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Pimalai Resort & Spa

Our choice of hotel on Koh Lanta. The three-storey private pool villa, separate lounge and kitchen building and wonderful sea views made this a fantastic family stay.

02

The private villa barbecue

Fresh seafood brought to the villa and cooked for us by the chefs. A memorable evening and one of the highlights of our stay.

03

Muay Thai

Getting up each morning with my son for Muay Thai classes was a brilliant way to start the day.

04

The beach

Vast stretches of sand and lovely walks along the coastline. Sometimes the simplest experiences are the ones you remember most.

05

Lanta Old Town

The east coast offers a different side of the island, with traditional wooden buildings and a quieter local atmosphere.

06

Mu Ko Lanta National Park

Forest, coastline and a wilder southern edge of the island for those days when you feel like exploring beyond the resort.

OUR EDIT

A wonderful villa. A slower island. Time together.

For us, Pimalai was the real highlight of Koh Lanta. The villa was outstanding, and having three levels of space meant the family could enjoy the holiday together without everyone having to occupy the same space all the time.

The children having their own floor, the private pool overlooking the sea and the separate lounge and kitchen building made it feel like a very special place to spend a few days.

We loved the little routines too. Muay Thai in the mornings with my son, long walks along the beach, fresh seafood cooked at the villa and evenings watching the sunset.

There were even a few unexpected visitors. Being robbed by monkeys one evening wasn't quite part of the plan, but it certainly added to the memories.

Koh Lanta reminded us that a good holiday doesn't always need a long list of activities. Sometimes a beautiful place to stay, a little time together and nowhere else to be is more than enough.
