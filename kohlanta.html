Koh Lanta

Slow days, warm water & island life.

OUR KOH LANTA

The island where doing very little felt like exactly the right thing.

Koh Lanta was never about racing around the island ticking things off. We found ourselves happily staying close to Pimalai, where the villa, pool, beach and warm Andaman water gave us more than enough for a few wonderfully slow days.

We had somewhere beautiful to retreat to, enough space for the whole family to relax and a rhythm that quickly became our own. On this trip, doing less wasn't a compromise. It was the point.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Koh Lanta, as we see it.

THE BASE

Our own little corner of the island.

We stayed at Pimalai Resort & Spa in a spacious duplex private pool villa, and it quickly became the centre of the holiday. It was the sort of place where lazy mornings, swims and long stretches of doing absolutely nothing felt completely natural.

SLOW DAYS

We didn't really need to go anywhere.

Days slipped naturally from the pool to the beach and back again. There was no pressure to make an itinerary work when the surroundings were already doing the job.

We would wander down towards the water, settle into the warmth and let the day unfold. It was the kind of holiday rhythm that is surprisingly difficult to manufacture — and wonderfully easy to fall into here.

THE ROUTINE

A little energy before the island slowed us down again.

Daily Muay Thai lessons gave the mornings some structure. It was a brilliant way to start the day — a little effort, plenty of energy, and then straight back into holiday mode.

LONG LUNCHES & DINNERS

Evenings were made for good food.

One of the highlights was having a chef come and cook for us on the terrace. It turned an ordinary evening at the villa into something memorable — good food, warm air and nowhere else we needed to be.

Meals were never rushed and evenings didn't need much planning. On Koh Lanta, the simplest plans often turned out to be the best ones.

BEST TIME

November – April

The dry season is generally the best time to enjoy Koh Lanta, with sunnier days and calmer seas.

THE FEELING

Slow & relaxed

Long beaches, warm water and a wonderfully unhurried island rhythm.

GETTING AROUND

Easy island life

There is plenty to explore, but Koh Lanta is equally rewarding when you decide not to go anywhere at all.

FROM THE JOURNAL

Sometimes the best island holiday is the one where you do less.

Koh Lanta wasn't the sort of place where we felt we needed to be out every morning discovering something new. Quite the opposite. We had arrived looking for an island escape, and Pimalai gave us exactly that.

Our duplex villa became our little world for a few days. There were swims, beach time, Muay Thai lessons, relaxed meals and afternoons where nobody seemed particularly interested in knowing what time it was.

Having a chef come and cook for us on the terrace was one of those small luxuries that stayed with us. Dinner stretched into the evening and there was no reason to go anywhere afterwards.

And perhaps that is what we remember most. Koh Lanta gave us permission to slow down. There was more of the island we could have explored, of course — but some holidays are better when you don't feel obliged to.
