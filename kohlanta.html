THAILAND

Koh Lanta

An island escape where beautiful beaches, jungle-covered hills and slow days come together in one of Thailand's quieter corners.

THE JOURNEY

Koh Lanta, our way

Koh Lanta felt like exactly the kind of island escape we were looking for. Beautiful beaches, warm water and a slower pace made it easy to settle into holiday mode without feeling that we needed to fill every day.

We stayed at Pimalai Resort & Spa in a spacious duplex private pool villa. The villa became our own little retreat, with plenty of room to relax and a terrace that quickly became one of our favourite places to spend time.

One of the highlights was having a chef come and cook for us on the terrace. Combined with daily Muay Thai lessons, swims and relaxed meals down the beach, the days developed an easy rhythm that suited Koh Lanta perfectly.

DESTINATION

Koh Lanta

A relaxed island in Thailand's Andaman Sea, known for long beaches, tropical scenery and a more laid-back atmosphere than some of Thailand's busier island destinations.

WHERE WE STAYED

Pimalai

Our base was Pimalai Resort & Spa, where our duplex private pool villa gave us plenty of space and a beautiful setting above the coast.

THE RHYTHM

Slow & Easy

Pool mornings, Muay Thai, beach time, long lunches and evenings that rarely needed much planning.

THE GUIDE

Koh Lanta, six ways

01

Stay somewhere special

Pimalai was a wonderful base for us. The private pool villa gave the stay a real sense of space and privacy while keeping the beach and resort facilities close at hand.

02

Make time for the pool

Koh Lanta rewards a slower approach. Leave a few hours with nothing planned, find a quiet spot and let the island do the rest.

03

Try Muay Thai

Daily Muay Thai lessons became part of our routine. It was a great way to start the day before cooling off in the pool and settling into breakfast.

04

Eat down the beach

Away from the resort, there are plenty of relaxed places to eat where the atmosphere is informal and the sea is never far away.

05

Have dinner at home

Having a chef cook on our villa terrace turned dinner into an occasion. With the setting already taken care of, there was nowhere else we needed to be.

06

Don't over-plan it

Koh Lanta is at its best when there is room in the day. A beach, a swim, something good to eat and nowhere you need to be can be more than enough.

OUR EDIT

The luxury of slowing down

Koh Lanta is not an island that needs to shout for your attention. Its appeal is in the space between things — the long stretches of coastline, the greenery behind the beaches and the feeling that the day does not really need a timetable.

For us, Pimalai made that feeling even easier to embrace. The villa was spacious enough to properly switch off, while the private pool and terrace gave us somewhere to spend the parts of the day when we simply didn't want to go anywhere.

There was still plenty of energy when we wanted it. Muay Thai brought a little structure to our mornings, and heading down the beach for something to eat gave us a taste of the more informal side of island life.

But perhaps the best thing about Koh Lanta was how little we felt we needed to do.

Sometimes the best island holiday is the one where the days simply disappear.
