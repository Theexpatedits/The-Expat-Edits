KOMODO JOURNEY

Komodo, Indonesia

Wild islands, extraordinary wildlife and three unforgettable nights aboard Samara 2 make Komodo one of the most rewarding journeys we've taken.

Begin Journey

The Experience

More than just the dragons

Komodo was one of those journeys that turned out to be about far more than we expected. We came for the famous dragons, but quickly discovered that the real magic lies in the islands, the water and the experience of exploring them by boat.

We split our time between the beautiful AYANA Komodo Waecicu Beach and three unforgettable nights aboard Samara 2. Being able to wake up somewhere completely different each morning, surrounded by turquoise water, dramatic islands and almost no signs of civilisation, made the trip feel genuinely special.

Of course, seeing Komodo dragons in their natural environment is unforgettable. But it was the combination of wildlife, incredible snorkelling, deserted beaches and life aboard Samara 2 that made this journey one we would happily do again.

01

The Dragons of Komodo

Seeing a Komodo dragon in the wild is every bit as extraordinary as you imagine it will be. These prehistoric-looking creatures move with an almost effortless calm, completely at home in a landscape that feels wonderfully untouched. Getting close enough to watch them in their natural environment was one of the defining moments of the journey.

02

Three Nights Aboard Samara 2

For us, the real luxury of Komodo was seeing the islands from the water. We spent three nights aboard Samara 2, waking somewhere different each morning and spending our days exploring remote beaches, hidden bays and the extraordinary coastline of the national park. It was slow travel at its very best.

03

Beneath the Surface

Komodo is just as spectacular beneath the water as it is above it. Clear turquoise seas, colourful reefs, turtles and the chance to snorkel alongside manta rays made the days on the water every bit as memorable as the wildlife on land. There is something very special about discovering these islands from both sides of the surface.

OUR BASE

AYANA Komodo Waecicu Beach

AYANA gave us the perfect base for exploring the Komodo region. Set on the waterfront with beautiful views across the surrounding islands, it is a luxurious place to slow down before or after heading out onto the water. The resort combines everything you would expect from AYANA with a wonderfully remote sense of place.

THE EXPERIENCE

Samara 2

For three nights we swapped the comfort of a resort for life aboard Samara 2. Exploring Komodo by traditional Indonesian sailing boat completely changes the experience. You wake up surrounded by islands, spend the day on the water and watch the sun disappear over the horizon before settling in for another night at sea. For us, this was the highlight of the trip.
