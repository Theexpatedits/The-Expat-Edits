OUR KOMODO

Komodo

Islands, ocean & one unforgettable adventure.

OUR KOMODO

One of our best trips.

Komodo was one of the best trips we've done. We used Ayana in Labuan Bajo as our base, with its beautiful pier stretching out into the sea. It was from there that Samara came to collect us, making the beginning of the adventure wonderfully easy.

Then we spent three days sailing between the islands, waking in a different bay each morning. We loved the remoteness of it — the feeling that the world had suddenly become islands, ocean and the next place waiting to be discovered.

Samara was fantastic. The staff were brilliant, the food was superb and everything was taken care of. It made the journey itself feel like part of the destination.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Komodo, as we saw it.

THE JOURNEY

Out on the water.

The great pleasure of Komodo is that the journey never feels separate from the destination. Every morning there was another bay, another island and another stretch of impossibly clear water waiting outside our cabin.

SAMARA

The boat was part of the adventure.

Samara gave the trip its rhythm. We would explore, swim and head ashore during the day, then return to the boat and watch another incredible landscape slip past. The staff were brilliant and the food was genuinely superb.

REMOTE

This is what we loved most.

There is something wonderfully disconnected about being out here. No roads, no schedule and very little between you and the islands. Just the boat, the sea and the next place on the horizon.

BELOW THE SURFACE

Another world underwater.

Some of our favourite moments happened in the water. The coral reefs were extraordinary, with turtles, manta rays and smaller marine life everywhere. At one stop, baby sharks were swimming between our feet.

THE ISLANDS

Pink beaches and extraordinary landscapes.

Pink Beach is exactly the kind of place that makes Komodo feel almost unreal. The colour of the sand, clear water and dry hills around it create a landscape unlike anywhere else we've travelled.

LIFE ABOARD

Wake somewhere new.

Three days on Samara meant that every morning began differently. We never quite knew what would be outside when we opened the door — another island, another bay, another reason to jump straight into the water.

PADAR ISLAND

The view is worth the climb.

Padar is one of those places that looks almost too perfect from above. The climb is worth it for the extraordinary view across its distinctive bays and the islands beyond.

WILDLIFE

Dragons, flying foxes and turtles.

The wildlife is another reason Komodo feels so special. We saw Komodo dragons in their natural environment, turtles in the water and flying foxes crossing the evening sky. It never felt like we were simply ticking things off a list — the wildlife was part of the place.

KOMODO DRAGONS

Seeing them where they belong.

Meeting the Komodo dragons in their natural environment was one of the moments that made the whole trip feel genuinely extraordinary. They are as impressive in person as you would imagine.

BACK ON LAND

Labuan Bajo.

Ayana made a beautiful base before and after the sailing trip. We also enjoyed getting out to Le Bajo, which gave us a different feel for Labuan Bajo away from the hotel.

THE MEMORY

A trip that was about more than the highlights.

Pink Beach, Padar, dragons, manta rays and coral reefs are the obvious memories. But what stays with us most is the feeling of being completely away from everything, moving slowly between islands and waking somewhere new.

BEST TIME

April – October

The dry season is generally the best time to explore the islands, particularly if you're planning to spend several days on the water.

WEATHER

Hot & dry

Warm, sunny conditions and dry island landscapes make Komodo feel completely different from much of tropical Indonesia.

GETTING AROUND

By boat

The real Komodo experience happens on the water. A liveaboard gives you the freedom to move between islands and wake somewhere new each morning.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places & experiences we loved.

01

Ayana

A beautiful base in Labuan Bajo. The huge pier stretching into the sea was particularly memorable and made our transfer onto Samara wonderfully easy.

02

Samara

The real highlight of the trip. Three days sailing between the islands, with excellent food, fantastic staff and a different bay waiting for us each morning.

03

Komodo dragons

Seeing the dragons in their natural environment was one of the experiences that made the trip feel truly special.

04

Pink Beach

Walking down onto Pink Beach was another highlight. The colour of the sand against the clear water makes for an extraordinary setting.

05

Padar Island

The climb is worth the effort. Looking out across the distinctive bays and surrounding islands gives you one of the great views of the whole trip.

06

Le Bajo

A great place to head out to when you're back in Labuan Bajo. It gave us a reason to leave the hotel and enjoy a different side of the town.

OUR EDIT

Stay on the water.

If you're coming to Komodo, we'd make the time to get properly out onto the water. For us, three days on Samara turned a beautiful destination into a genuinely unforgettable trip.

Waking in a different bay each morning, heading ashore to explore and then returning to the boat made the journey itself the experience. Add in the coral reefs, turtles, manta rays, dragons and sheer remoteness, and it is hard to imagine a better way to see these islands.

OUR KOMODO

Komodo

OUR KOMODO

One of our best trips.

Komodo was one of the best trips we've done. We used Ayana in Labuan Bajo as our base, a hugely picturesque hotel with a beautiful pier stretching out into the sea. It was from that pier that Samara came to collect us, making the start of our adventure incredibly easy.

We then spent three days sailing around the region, waking up in a different bay each morning and watching the landscape change around us. The islands, clear water and dry hills were stunning, but it was the feeling of being out on the water that really made the trip.

Samara was fantastic. The staff were brilliant, the food was excellent and everything was taken care of. Days were spent exploring the islands, swimming and taking in the scenery, before returning to the boat and waking somewhere completely different the next morning.

We saw Komodo dragons, walked down Pink Beach and climbed Padar Island, but some of our favourite memories are simply waking up surrounded by another beautiful bay. Back in Labuan Bajo, Le Bajo was a great place to head out to away from the hotel.

We also visited the Marriott, but found it a little too quiet for us. In the end we were very pleased that we'd chosen Ayana. The combination of the hotel, the pier and three days on Samara made this a trip we'll remember for a long time.
