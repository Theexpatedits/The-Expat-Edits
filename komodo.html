INDONESIA

Komodo

Islands, ocean and wild beauty.

The Journey

Into the islands

Komodo is one of those places where the landscape does much of the talking. Dry hills fall into impossibly clear water, small islands appear and disappear on the horizon, and days are largely shaped by the sea.

We split our time between Ayana in Labuan Bajo and Samara, our liveaboard, which became the real highlight of the trip.

01

Labuan Bajo

We began in Labuan Bajo, using Ayana as our base before heading out onto the water. The hotel was comfortable, the rooms were beautiful and the welcome was particularly warm.

One of the details we appreciated most was that Ayana allowed Samara to collect us directly from their own pier, making the transition from hotel to liveaboard remarkably easy.

02

Out on the water

Once we left Labuan Bajo, the experience changed completely. The landscape becomes a succession of islands, reefs and quiet stretches of turquoise water.

From the camera

Islands & ocean

Where we stayed

Samara

Samara was the real highlight of the trip. As a liveaboard, it allowed us to experience Komodo properly rather than simply visiting it from shore.

We couldn't speak more highly of the experience. The food was excellent, the staff were fantastic and the whole operation felt incredibly well looked after. Once on board, everything was taken care of.

Stay

Ayana

Ayana was our base before heading out onto Samara. The rooms were beautiful and the hotel made us feel very welcome from the start.

Its own pier was particularly useful for us, as it meant Samara could collect us directly from the hotel. For a stay around Labuan Bajo, that made the logistics wonderfully straightforward.

Eat & Drink

Le Bajo

We ate at Le Bajo a couple of times during the trip, having both lunch and dinner there outside of Ayana.

It was one of those places that became part of the trip rather than simply somewhere to eat — a great setting on the pier and well worth seeking out while you're in Labuan Bajo.

Another option

Marriott

We also visited the Marriott for lunch. It was a perfectly pleasant stop, although for us it felt a little too quiet compared with the atmosphere we enjoyed elsewhere.

Don't Miss

The Komodo experience

01

Get out on the water

The best way to experience Komodo is to spend time among the islands rather than simply using Labuan Bajo as a base.

02

Stay on a liveaboard

For us, Samara was the standout. Having everything taken care of while moving through the islands made the trip feel effortless.

03

Leave time for the landscape

Komodo isn't somewhere to rush. The quiet bays, dry hills, turquoise water and changing light are as much a part of the experience as the individual sights.

Our Take

Wild, easy and worth doing properly.

Komodo is at its best when you give yourself enough time to get away from the mainland and let the islands become the trip. For us, the combination of Ayana and Samara worked particularly well — comfort at either end, with the real adventure in between.
