INDONESIA

Lombok

Beaches, islands and the slower side of Indonesia.

THE JOURNEY

An island made for exploring.

Lombok is a great island to explore and we loved getting to all parts of it. Rather than staying in one place, we moved around the island and found that each part had something different to offer.

We started in the south east at Lobster Bay, a private villa resort with some amazing sunsets. From there we moved west along the southern coast to Segara, another superb boutique stay with some great food.

Further north, the Oberoi gave us a great base for exploring the three Gili Islands. We spent time on all three, hopping between them by spider boat, before enjoying the laid-back atmosphere of Kuta.

OUR EDIT

Don't stay in one place.

Lombok is an island we'd recommend exploring properly. We loved getting to different parts of it rather than simply choosing one resort and staying there.

Lobster Bay in the south east was a highlight, particularly for the sunsets, while Segara gave us another superb stop as we moved west along the southern coast.

Further north, the Oberoi worked brilliantly as a base for the Gilis. We loved jumping between all three by spider boat, with Pondok Santi on Gili T providing exactly the laid-back beach atmosphere we were looking for.

And in Kuta, we were very happy simply sitting in coffee shops and watching the world go by. Tribe was perfect for that, particularly with the skateboarders providing the entertainment.
