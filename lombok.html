INDONESIA

Lombok

Beaches, islands & the slower side of Indonesia.

OUR LOMBOK

An island made for exploring. And somewhere we kept wanting to return to.

Lombok is an island we loved exploring. Rather than choosing one hotel and staying put, we moved around, discovering different coastlines, little places to eat and some wonderful spots to stay along the way.

We began in the south east at Lobster Bay, a family-run villa retreat where our own private pool and the walk down to a secluded beach made it very easy to settle in. From there we travelled west to Segara, a small collection of bungalows with a lovely beachfront restaurant.

Further north, The Oberoi gave us a base for discovering the three Gili Islands. We loved jumping between them by spider boat, before enjoying the quieter side of Gili T at Pondok Santi.

And then there was Kuta. Coffee shops, surfboards, skateboarders, beach stops and the boys learning to surf. Lombok gave us a little bit of everything, and that is exactly what made it such a memorable trip.

THE FIRST STAY · SOUTH EAST LOMBOK

Lobster Bay.

A private villa retreat, a beautiful setting and the sort of welcome that makes you feel at home almost immediately.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A lovely villa, a private pool and a warm welcome.

We started our time in Lombok at Lobster Bay in the south east. We loved the villa, particularly having our own private pool. It gave us the space to relax and enjoy the setting at our own pace.

The location was another big part of its appeal. There was a lovely long walk down to a secluded beach, which made getting out and exploring the coast feel like part of the experience.

What really stood out, though, was the hospitality. It was a family-run place and they made us feel genuinely welcome, almost as though we were staying with people we already knew.

It was a wonderful way to begin the trip. The sunsets were a particular highlight, and we were very happy to spend time simply enjoying the villa and its surroundings.

For anyone looking for a more private, personal stay away from the busier parts of the island, Lobster Bay is somewhere we would happily recommend.

THE SETTING

Space to slow down.

One of the things we enjoyed most was the feeling of having our own little corner of Lombok. The greenery, the open views and the walk towards the beach all added to the appeal.

It was a lovely contrast to the places we would discover further along the coast.

THE SECOND STAY · SOUTHERN COAST

Segara.

A small boutique stay with comfortable bungalows, a wonderful beachfront restaurant and a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

OUR EXPERIENCE

Small, relaxed and very easy to enjoy.

Moving west along the southern coast brought us to Segara. It was a very small property, made up of individual bungalows, and we really enjoyed the intimate feel of the place.

The restaurant was a real highlight. Sitting right on the beach, it served some lovely food, including healthy dishes that were both fresh and full of flavour.

The pool setting was another lovely feature. It was the sort of place where you could spend a relaxed afternoon, then wander down to the beach for a swim or to watch the surf.

The beachfront also offered some good surfing, which added another dimension to the stay. Segara felt different from Lobster Bay, but that was part of what made moving around the island so enjoyable.

FURTHER NORTH · THE GILI ISLANDS

The Oberoi, the Gilis and Pondok Santi.

Heading north brought a completely different side of Lombok. From our base at The Oberoi, we could explore all three Gili Islands before settling into the slower pace of Gili T.

OUR GILI EXPERIENCE

Three islands, and a favourite place to stay.

The Oberoi was a great base for exploring the three Gili Islands. We loved being able to get out on the water and jump between the islands by spider boat.

Each has its own personality, and spending time on all three is well worth doing rather than assuming they are much the same.

On Gili T, Pondok Santi was a particular highlight. It is a small, family-owned boutique property on a quieter side of the island, and we thought it was superb.

The accommodation was very well appointed, the reef outside was gorgeous and the beachfront dining had a wonderful atmosphere. Chilled music, good food and a relaxed feel made it somewhere we could happily spend a lot of time.

Pondok Santi is a massive recommendation from us if you are staying on Gili T.

BEYOND THE HOTELS

Discovering Lombok.

THE JOURNEY

Half the fun is seeing what happens next.

One of the things we loved about Lombok was getting out and exploring. Travelling around the island brings plenty of little surprises, and not everything needs to be planned.

We even found ourselves sharing the road with buffalo. These are the sorts of moments that make a journey feel like your own.

SIMPLE STOPS

Good food, cold drinks and somewhere to sit.

Some of our favourite stops were the simple ones. Little beach places, somewhere to sit in the shade and a cold drink while looking out over the water.

There is no need to make everything complicated. That laid-back approach is part of Lombok's charm.

KUTA

Coffee, cafés and a little island energy.

We really loved Kuta. It has a relaxed, youthful feel and is somewhere we were very happy simply spending time.

We enjoyed sitting in coffee shops, watching the town go by and discovering the little places that give the area its character.

SURF & SEA

Learning the rhythm of the coast.

We loved watching the boys learning to surf. There is something very enjoyable about spending time around the water, whether you are getting in yourself or simply watching from the beach.

The coastline around Kuta gives the whole area a lovely surf culture.

THE GILI ISLANDS

Clear water, reefs and island days.

The Gilis were one of the highlights of our time in Lombok. We loved exploring by boat, swimming in the clear water and discovering the reefs around the islands.

We saw turtles and enjoyed the slower pace on land just as much as the time in the water.

THE EVENINGS

Time to slow down.

Some of the best memories are not necessarily the big excursions. They are the evenings, the changing light and the time spent watching the sun disappear over the water.

Lombok gave us plenty of those moments.

THE JOURNEY CONTINUES

More moments from Lombok.

THE ISLAND FEEL

Beautiful places and little discoveries.

From the quieter beaches to the busy little corners of the island, we loved the variety of experiences Lombok offered.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

When to go and how to make the most of it.

Lombok is an island that rewards a little planning, but we would also leave plenty of room for spontaneous stops and exploring.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

May – October

The dry season is generally the best time to explore Lombok, with sunnier weather and good conditions for beach days and getting out around the island.

July and August are popular holiday months, so accommodation can be busier.

WEATHER & CONDITIONS

Warm and tropical

Lombok is warm throughout the year. The drier months generally bring more reliable sunshine, while the wetter season usually falls between November and March.

Conditions can vary, particularly for surfing and boat trips.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Seven days or more

We would allow enough time to enjoy the different parts of the island rather than rushing between them.

A few nights in the south, time further along the coast and a couple of days exploring the Gilis makes a lovely trip.

GETTING THERE

Via Lombok Airport

Lombok International Airport is the main arrival point. From there, the southern coast and other parts of the island can be reached by road.

GETTING AROUND

Explore by road and boat

We enjoyed moving between different parts of Lombok by road. For the Gili Islands, boats are an essential part of the experience.

Allow time for the journeys rather than trying to fit too much into one day.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Families, couples and explorers

Lombok works particularly well for people who enjoy a mix of comfortable stays, beaches, good food, surfing and getting out to explore.

It is a wonderful alternative to a conventional resort holiday.

OUR EDIT

Don't stay in one place.

Lombok is an island we'd recommend exploring properly. We loved moving around and experiencing different parts of it rather than choosing one resort and staying there for the entire trip.

Lobster Bay in the south east was a wonderful place to begin. The private villa, pool, secluded beach and warm family hospitality made it a particularly memorable stay.

Moving west to Segara gave us another lovely experience. Small bungalows, a great beachfront restaurant, healthy food and a relaxed pool setting. It was a different feel, but one we enjoyed just as much.

Further north, The Oberoi worked brilliantly as a base for exploring the Gilis. We loved jumping between all three by spider boat, seeing turtles and discovering the different character of each island. Pondok Santi on Gili T was a real highlight.

And then there was Kuta. We loved the coffee shops, the skateboarders at Tribe, the boys learning to surf and the simple pleasure of sitting somewhere and watching the world go by.

For us, Lombok was about variety. Beautiful places to stay, great food, time on the water and plenty of little discoveries along the way. If we were planning the trip again, we would do exactly the same thing: move around, take our time and enjoy each part of the island.
