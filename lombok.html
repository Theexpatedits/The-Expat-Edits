LOMBOK & THE GILI ISLANDS

Lombok & the Gili Islands

Clear turquoise water, quiet beaches, colourful island life and a slower side of Indonesia that is very easy to fall in love with.

Begin Journey

The Experience

Island life at a slower pace

Lombok and the Gili Islands feel like the sort of place where the pace of life naturally begins to slow. Days are shaped by the water, the weather and whatever happens to be around the next corner.

The Gilis bring the classic tropical island experience, with impossibly clear water, colourful boats and an easygoing atmosphere. Back on Lombok, the landscape becomes bigger and more dramatic, with beaches, mountains, villages and a wonderfully relaxed sense of space.

What we loved most was the contrast. One moment you're cycling through the streets of a tiny island, the next you're swimming in crystal-clear water or watching the sun disappear over the horizon. It feels wonderfully uncomplicated.

01

Life on the Gilis

The Gili Islands are wonderfully simple. There are no cars, the roads are made for bicycles and the pace is dictated more by the sea than the clock. Cycling around the islands, stopping for a swim and watching the boats come and go is exactly the sort of travel that makes you forget what time it is.

02

The Lombok Landscape

Away from the islands, Lombok feels bigger, quieter and more dramatic. Beautiful beaches sit beneath green hills and mountains, while the roads lead through villages and countryside where everyday life continues at an unhurried pace. It is a side of Indonesia that feels wonderfully authentic.

03

Sunset Over the Islands

The days tend to finish as beautifully as they begin. As the light fades across the water, the islands take on a completely different character. Whether watching from the beach or simply sitting somewhere with a view, sunset is one of the easiest reasons to slow down and stay a little longer.

THE ISLANDS

The Gili Islands

The Gilis are all about slowing down. With no cars, beautiful beaches and some of the clearest water in Indonesia, they are the perfect place to spend a few days swimming, cycling and exploring at your own pace.

THE MAINLAND

Lombok

Lombok offers a completely different sense of scale. The island's beaches, hills and villages provide a quieter and more expansive experience, with plenty of opportunities to explore beyond the better-known tourist routes.
