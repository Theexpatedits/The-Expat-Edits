INDONESIA

Lombok

Beaches, islands and the slower side of Indonesia.

THE JOURNEY

An island made for exploring.

Lombok is a great island to explore and we loved getting out and seeing all parts of it. Rather than staying in one place, we moved around the island, discovering very different sides of Lombok along the way.

We started in the south east at a private villa resort called Lobster Bay, before moving west along the southern coast to Segara. From there we headed north towards the Gili Islands, using the Oberoi as our base.

The three Gilis were completely different again, and we loved hopping between them by spider boat. Then there was Kuta, where the pace slowed right down — coffee shops, skateboards and watching the world go by.

LOMBOK

Take the long way around.

SOUTH EAST LOMBOK

Lobster Bay

We started our Lombok trip in the south east at Lobster Bay, a private villa resort that we would highly recommend.

The setting was beautiful, but the sunsets were the real highlight. It was a fantastic place to start the trip and immediately gave us that sense of being somewhere properly removed from everyday life.

SOUTHERN LOMBOK

Keep moving west.

OUR EDIT

Make the journey part of the trip.

From Lobster Bay we moved west along the south coast and stayed at Segara, another boutique hotel that we thought was superb.

The restaurant was particularly good, making it one of those places where you don't necessarily feel the need to go looking elsewhere every night. It was a great stop as we continued exploring the island.

One of the things we enjoyed most about Lombok was moving around. Each stop felt different, and the journey between them was part of what made the island so enjoyable.

THE GILI ISLANDS

Three islands. Three different moods.

THE GILIS

Get on a spider boat.

We stayed further north at the Oberoi because of its proximity to the Gili Islands, which made it a great base for exploring them.

We visited all three of the Gilis, moving between them by spider boat. Each had a different feel, which is exactly why we enjoyed seeing all three rather than settling on just one.

ISLAND LIFE

Take your time.

KUTA

Coffee, skateboards and watching the world go by.

Kuta has a completely different atmosphere again. We loved sitting in the coffee shops, taking our time and simply watching the world go by.

Tribe was a particular favourite. It's a great place to sit with a coffee and watch the skateboarders, which pretty much sums up the relaxed character of Kuta for us.

KUTA LOMBOK

Slow down.

BEST TIME

Dry season

The drier months generally make exploring the island, beaches and Gilis easier.

WEATHER

Tropical

Expect warm temperatures and plenty of sunshine, with seasonal rain.

GETTING AROUND

Move around

Lombok rewards exploring beyond a single base, with boats useful for reaching the Gilis.

OUR EDIT

Don't just stay in one place.

Lombok is an island we'd recommend exploring properly. We loved moving from the south east across the coast, heading north to the Gilis and then spending time in Kuta. The stays, beaches and islands all felt different, and that variety is what made the trip special for us.
