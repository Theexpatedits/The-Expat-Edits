INDONESIA

Lombok

Beaches, islands & the slower side of Indonesia.

OUR LOMBOK

An island made for exploring.

Lombok is a great island to explore and we loved getting to all parts of it. Rather than staying in one place, we moved around the island and found that each part had something different to offer.

We started in the south east at Lobster Bay, a private villa resort with some amazing sunsets. From there we moved west along the southern coast to Segara, another superb boutique hotel with some great food at its restaurant.

Further north, the Oberoi gave us a great base for exploring the three Gili Islands. We loved popping between all three by spider boat, before spending time in Kuta, sitting in coffee shops and watching the world go by.

OUR EDIT

Don't stay in one place.

Lombok is an island we'd recommend exploring properly. We loved getting to different parts of it rather than simply choosing one resort and staying there.

Lobster Bay in the south east was a highlight, particularly for the sunsets. Moving west to Segara gave us another superb stay and some great food along the way.

Further north, the Oberoi worked brilliantly as a base for the Gili Islands. We loved jumping between all three by spider boat, with Pondok Santi on Gili T providing exactly the laid-back beach atmosphere we were looking for.

And in Kuta, we were very happy simply sitting in coffee shops and watching the world go by. Tribe was perfect for that, particularly with the skateboarders providing the entertainment.
