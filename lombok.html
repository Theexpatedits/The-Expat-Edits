INDONESIA

Lombok

Islands, beaches and the slower side of Indonesia.

The Journey

Islands, beaches and slow days.

Lombok gave us exactly the kind of escape we were looking for. We based ourselves near the Gili islands, spent time moving between them by boat and then headed south to explore another side of Lombok.

Each of the Gilis had its own feel and we enjoyed them all for different reasons — from snorkelling off Gili Air to the chilled beach atmosphere of Gili T.

Further south, Kuta and the surrounding coastline felt quieter and more relaxed, with beautiful beaches, small hotels and places that were easy to settle into.

01

Start near the Gilis

We stayed at the Oberoi, up towards the Gili islands, which gave us a great base for getting out onto the water.

From there we could jump on a local boat and explore the islands, each of which had its own personality and atmosphere.

02

Every island feels different

The great thing about the Gilis is that none of them felt quite the same. We loved each one for different reasons and it was worth taking the time to explore rather than choosing just one.

From the camera

The Gili islands

Gili Air

Into the water

Gili Air was one of our favourite stops, particularly for the snorkelling. The water is beautifully clear and getting into it is one of the best ways to experience the island.

Days here are uncomplicated — swimming, snorkelling, eating and simply enjoying the pace of island life.

Gili T

Pondok Santi

We stayed at Pondok Santi on Gili T and loved the chilled atmosphere. It was exactly the kind of place where you could slow down, spend time by the beach and forget about the clock.

The beach bar was a particular favourite and gave the whole stay an easy, relaxed feel.

Island life

Slow days on the Gilis

03

Head south

From the Gilis we headed south and found a completely different side of Lombok. The coastline felt quieter, the beaches were beautiful and there was much more space to simply slow down.

South Lombok

Lobster Bay

Lobster Bay was gorgeous and became one of our favourite places on the southern side of the island. It had the kind of setting that makes it very easy to forget what time it is.

Stay

Segara

We also stayed at Segara, which offered small, comfortable huts and a wonderfully uncomplicated atmosphere.

It was the sort of place that suited Lombok perfectly: comfortable without feeling overdone and a good base for exploring the area around Kuta.

Kuta Lombok

Coffee & skateboards

Eat & Drink

Tribe

We loved Kuta and would definitely recommend stopping at Tribe for a coffee.

The great thing about it was the setting — you could sit back with a coffee and watch the skateboarders while the town moved around you.

Our Take

Easy, beautiful and very easy to love.

Lombok worked for us because there was no need to rush. The Gilis gave us clear water, boats and island life, while the south offered beautiful beaches, relaxed places to stay and the easy atmosphere of Kuta. It's the kind of trip where the slower days become the best ones.
