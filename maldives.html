OUR MALDIVES

Maldives

A private island, extraordinary water and life at a slower pace.

OUR MALDIVES

More than just paradise.

We stayed at One&Only Reethi Rah, and it was one of those places where the setting makes you slow down almost immediately. We had a beach villa, a full-time butler and the good fortune of being in the very last bungalow along our stretch of the island.

Our private pool looked out across a beautiful stretch of sea, with no passing footfall beyond our own little corner. We were told that the end villa had previously been enjoyed by the Beckhams, although the real appeal for us was simply the privacy.

Days were spent cycling beneath the palms, looking out for flying foxes and herons, and getting into the water. The diving and snorkelling were every bit as memorable as the island itself.

THE STAY · ONE&ONLY REETHI RAH

Our own private corner of the island.

A sublime beach villa, a full-time butler and a private stretch of shoreline that made it very easy to switch off.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A beach villa with a wonderful sense of privacy.

Our beach villa was sublime. We were lucky enough to have the end bungalow, meaning there was no footfall beyond our own private stretch of island. It felt wonderfully secluded without being cut off from everything the resort had to offer.

The private pool looked straight across a beautiful stretch of sea. It was the sort of view that made staying in feel every bit as appealing as heading out to explore.

Having a full-time butler made the whole stay feel effortless. Everything was taken care of, leaving us free to enjoy the island at our own pace.

One evening we arranged a private barbecue on the beach. With the sea just beyond us, it was a lovely way to spend an evening and one of the memories that really captures what made the stay special.

Our verdict: the villa, the privacy, the pool and the service made this an exceptional place to spend time together.

THE SETTING

Room to disappear for a while.

OUR LITTLE CORNER

Just the sea, the sand and the sound of the island.

What we particularly loved about our position was the feeling of having our own little piece of Reethi Rah. Being at the end meant that nobody was walking past our villa to get somewhere else.

The pool and the view became a natural part of the day, whether we were returning from exploring or simply deciding to spend a little longer at home.

THE ARRIVAL

Arriving by yacht.

FROM MALÉ TO REETHI RAH

A lovely way to begin the stay.

We used the yacht to get us to and from the island from Malé, which was a particularly nice touch. Rather than simply arriving by the usual transfer, the journey itself became part of the experience.

There is something special about approaching an island resort from the water. It sets the tone before you have even reached the villa, and makes the transition from everyday life feel complete.

Once on the island, we quickly settled into a much slower rhythm: bicycles, beaches, the pool and plenty of time in the sea.

THE ISLAND

Reethi Rah, as we saw it.

ISLAND LIFE

The pleasure of two wheels.

Cycling around the island quickly became part of our daily routine. It is a lovely way to explore without having to be anywhere in particular.

The paths take you beneath the palms and through the greenery, with different beaches and views appearing as you go.

WILDLIFE

Look beyond the shoreline.

I spent a surprising amount of time looking into the trees for flying foxes and watching the birds around the island.

Herons became familiar characters during our stay, and the flying foxes were another reminder that there is plenty happening beyond the beach.

EVENINGS

Time to do very little.

Not every day needed an activity. Some of the nicest moments came from watching the light change, spending time by the pool and enjoying the feeling of being somewhere so far removed from the usual routine.

BENEATH THE SURFACE

The water was every bit as good as the island.

THE DIVING

They knew exactly where to take us.

The diving was great. The team would drop us into exactly the right spot depending on what we were hoping to see, which made a real difference to the experience.

We saw an extraordinary variety of marine life, and it was a completely different way to appreciate the Maldives beyond the beaches and lagoons.

SNORKELLING

Thousands of parrot fish.

Even snorkelling just off the atoll was great fun. On one occasion I found myself surrounded by thousands of parrot fish. It was one of those unexpected encounters that makes you stop and take in what is happening around you.

You do not always need to head out on a boat to find something remarkable. There was plenty happening just beneath the surface close to the island.

REEF LIFE

A whole world in the shallows.

The reef offered plenty more to look at, from colourful fish moving through the coral to smaller details that were easy to miss at first.

It was a lovely contrast to the larger encounters on the dives, and a reminder to take your time when exploring the water.

PLANNING YOUR STAY

A few thoughts before you go.

Reethi Rah is a private island resort, so the experience is as much about enjoying the property and the water as it is about getting out to explore.

BEST TIME

November – April

The dry season generally brings sunnier skies and calmer seas. Conditions vary, but this is a popular period for enjoying the islands and getting out on the water.

GETTING THERE

Arrive by sea

We travelled between Malé and Reethi Rah by yacht. It was a lovely way to arrive and added a sense of occasion to the start of the stay.

GETTING AROUND

By bicycle

Cycling was one of our favourite ways to explore the island. It is easy to enjoy the different corners of the resort without rushing.

OUR EDIT

A private island, with plenty to discover.

The Maldives is easy to reduce to photographs of beaches and turquoise water. Reethi Rah certainly has those in abundance, but what stayed with us was the variety of the experience.

Our beach villa was sublime, particularly being at the end of the island with our own sense of privacy. The full-time butler, the pool looking out to sea and the private barbecue on the beach all made the stay feel wonderfully effortless.

Beyond the villa, we loved cycling around the island, looking for flying foxes and herons, and heading out into the water. The diving was excellent, but the snorkelling was just as memorable in its own way. Being surrounded by thousands of parrot fish is not something you forget in a hurry.

Add in the yacht transfer from Malé and the whole stay felt special from beginning to end. It was a lovely balance of adventure, privacy and doing very little at all.

OUR MAP

One&Only Reethi Rah, Maldives.
