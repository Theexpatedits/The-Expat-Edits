Penang

Walk, eat and discover George Town.

OUR PENANG

Walk, eat, discover.

We loved walking around Penang. George Town is the sort of place that rewards you for taking your time, with beautiful streets, small shops, street art and something interesting appearing around almost every corner.

We stayed at The Blue Mansion — Cheong Fatt Tze, which gave us a great base for exploring the city. But it was really the combination of wandering and eating that made Penang work so well for us.

We had an amazing dinner at Peninsula House and particularly enjoyed hearing about their vegetable growing room. We ate at the communal table and then stayed for some after-dinner wine upstairs. It was one of those meals that became part of the experience rather than simply somewhere to eat.

We also enjoyed a nice lunch at the Eastern & Oriental Hotel and spent a day up on Penang Hill, where we had fantastic views over the city and saw plenty of wildlife and monkeys.

OUR EDIT

Leave plenty of time to wander.

What we loved most about Penang was how easy it was to spend a day without much of a plan. We would walk the streets of George Town, stop to look at street art, wander into small shops and eventually find somewhere good to eat.

Peninsula House was a particular highlight. The vegetable growing room was fascinating to hear about, and sitting at the communal table before heading upstairs for some after-dinner wine made the whole evening feel very personal.

Then there was Penang Hill, which gave us a completely different perspective on the city. The views were fantastic, but the wildlife and monkeys made the day just as memorable.
