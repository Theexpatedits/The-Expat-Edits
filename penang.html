MALAYSIA

Penang

Walking, eating and discovering George Town.

The Experience

Walk, eat, discover

Penang is a place that rewards you for slowing down.

We loved simply walking around, discovering the streets of George Town and seeing what was around the next corner. The architecture, small shops, old buildings and everyday life give the city a character that is difficult to capture unless you explore it on foot.

And then there is the food.

Eating became a big part of the experience for us, with some genuinely memorable meals along the way. Two of the places we'd particularly recommend are Community Table and Peninsula House.

Penang felt like the sort of place where you don't need a packed itinerary. Just put on your shoes, head outside and start walking.

01

The best way to see Penang

We found ourselves walking almost everywhere. George Town is full of little details that are easy to miss when you're moving from one attraction to the next.

The pleasure was simply wandering and seeing what we found.

02

Around every corner

Old shop houses, colourful buildings, street scenes and small details made the city feel incredibly photogenic.

Penang is somewhere where the journey between places can be just as enjoyable as the destination itself.

03

Come for the food

Food was a big part of why we enjoyed Penang so much. Community Table and Peninsula House were two of our favourite recommendations from the trip and both are places we'd happily return to.

Eat & Drink

Our Penang picks

Community Table was one of our favourite meals in Penang. A restaurant worth seeking out if good food is high on your list when travelling.

Peninsula House was another place we really enjoyed and one we would recommend adding to a Penang food itinerary.

Our Take

Penang is best explored without much of a plan.

What we enjoyed most about Penang was the freedom to simply walk, eat and discover. George Town has enough character to keep you interested around every corner, while the food gives you plenty of reasons to keep wandering. It's the sort of place where the days can unfold naturally, which is exactly how we like to travel.
