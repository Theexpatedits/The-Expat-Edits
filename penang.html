Penang

A colourful island where heritage streets, incredible food and rich cultural history come together to create one of Malaysia's most rewarding destinations.

Begin Journey

The Experience

Malaysia's cultural capital

Penang surprised us from the moment we arrived. George Town is one of those places where every street seems to tell a different story, with colourful heritage buildings, hidden cafés, temples and street art around almost every corner.

What makes Penang so memorable is its mix of cultures. Malay, Chinese and Indian influences have shaped everything from the architecture to the world-famous food, creating a destination that feels unlike anywhere else in Southeast Asia.

Whether you're wandering through UNESCO-listed streets, sampling local hawker food or escaping into the hills above the city, Penang offers a wonderful balance of history, culture and relaxed island life.

01

George Town

The heart of Penang is George Town, a UNESCO World Heritage city where every street feels like an open-air museum. Beautiful colonial buildings, colourful shophouses and hidden courtyards make it a place that's best explored on foot, with something interesting waiting around almost every corner.

02

Street Art & Local Life

One of our favourite things about Penang was simply wandering. The famous street art, traditional trishaws, local markets and endless cafés give the city its unique personality. Every lane seemed to reveal another mural, temple or place to stop for something delicious.

03

Beyond The Old Town

While George Town steals most of the attention, Penang has plenty more to discover. From panoramic viewpoints and peaceful temples to tropical gardens and the island's quieter coastline, it's well worth slowing down and exploring beyond the historic centre.
