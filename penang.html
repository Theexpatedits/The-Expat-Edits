OUR PENANG

Penang

A city made for wandering, eating & discovering.

OUR PENANG

A beautiful place to stay. A city full of character.

We loved Penang. George Town is the sort of place that rewards you for taking your time, with beautiful streets, old shophouses, street art and something interesting appearing around almost every corner.

We stayed at the Blue Mansion, a wonderfully appointed hotel that gave us a memorable base for exploring the city. After long days on foot, it was lovely to return for a drink at the bar.

There was plenty beyond the old town, too. A day up on Penang Hill brought wonderful views across the city and the unexpected pleasure of seeing monkeys in the trees. And the food scene gave us some of our favourite evenings of the trip.

THE STAY

The Blue Mansion.

A characterful place to call home in George Town, and a lovely place to return to after a day exploring.

OUR BASE IN GEORGE TOWN

A little time to slow down.

The Blue Mansion was a great fit for our stay. It was beautifully appointed and had the sort of atmosphere that made coming back at the end of the day feel like part of the experience.

OUR EXPERIENCE

Drinks after a day in the streets.

We spent much of our time exploring George Town on foot, wandering through the old streets, looking at the shophouses and discovering temples, street art and all the little details that make the city so distinctive.

After those long days out, we particularly enjoyed returning to the Blue Mansion and settling in for drinks at the bar. It was a relaxed way to finish the day before heading out for dinner or simply enjoying the atmosphere of the hotel.

The Blue Mansion felt very much in keeping with the city around it. It gave us a comfortable and memorable base, without taking us away from the pleasure of discovering George Town.

Our advice would be to choose somewhere with character in the heart of George Town. Being able to explore on foot was a big part of what made our time in Penang so enjoyable.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering George Town.

GEORGE TOWN

A city made for wandering.

Our favourite way to see George Town was simply on foot. We would head out without much of a plan and let the streets decide where we went next.

Old shophouses, colourful facades and beautiful little details give the city a character that is difficult to reproduce anywhere else.

STREET ART

Always something around the corner.

One of the pleasures of walking George Town was spotting the street art. It never felt like we were following a checklist. There was always another piece appearing as we wandered through the city.

THE OLD CITY

Look a little closer.

The shopfronts, signs, colours and layers of history make this a wonderfully photogenic place. The pleasure is in moving slowly enough to notice them.

TEMPLES & LANDMARKS

Colour, detail and history.

The temples and religious buildings we came across added another layer to the city. Their decoration, lanterns and intricate details were wonderful to explore.

THE DETAILS

A city with its own rhythm.

Some of our favourite moments were the small discoveries: a building we hadn't noticed before, a beautiful facade or a street that invited us to take a different route.

UP IN THE HILLS

A different view of Penang.

Penang Hill was one of our favourite days. The views back over the whole city were fantastic and gave us a completely different perspective on George Town.

And then there were the monkeys. Seeing them while walking through the hills made the day feel much more like an adventure than a sightseeing trip.

THE WILDLIFE

An unexpected highlight.

The views were reason enough to make the trip up the hill, but seeing monkeys while walking through the trees made the day even more memorable.

It was a lovely change of pace from the streets and buildings of George Town.

A CHANGE OF PACE

Above the city.

Penang Hill gave us a chance to step away from the busy streets and see the island from another perspective. We would definitely make time for it again.

AT THE TABLE

Some very memorable evenings.

Food was a big part of what made Penang work for us. Two evenings in particular stand out, each for quite different reasons.

OUR FAVOURITE RESTAURANT

Peninsula House.

Peninsula House was probably our favourite restaurant in Penang. It had exactly the mix we enjoy: relaxed but elegant, with a lovely atmosphere and a real sense of care behind the food.

What particularly interested us was its eco-grow operation in-house, providing fresh vegetables and ingredients for the kitchen. It was a lovely idea and made the experience feel even more thoughtful.

It was the sort of restaurant where you could settle in, enjoy the evening and not feel hurried. We would happily return.

DINNER & A NIGHTCAP

Community Table.

We also had a brilliant evening at Community Table. Dinner was served along a long counter, where we could sit and watch the chefs working in front of us.

But the evening didn't finish when dinner did. Afterwards, we were taken upstairs to a great wine bar to continue the night.

That combination of an engaging dinner and somewhere lovely to finish with a glass of wine made it another memorable Penang evening.

More moments from Penang.

THE STREET SCENES

Always worth another look.

From the old shopfronts to the colourful streets and the details of everyday life, George Town kept giving us reasons to stop and take another photograph.

A FINAL IMPRESSION

A place that stays with you.

We left Penang with memories of beautiful streets, a wonderful hotel, great food and a day in the hills that gave us a different view of the city.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

Making the most of Penang.

Penang is a destination that rewards allowing time to explore. These are a few thoughts based on how we enjoyed our stay.

WHERE TO STAY

George Town

We stayed at the Blue Mansion and loved having a characterful base in the heart of the city. Staying centrally makes it easy to explore the streets on foot.

HOW TO EXPLORE

Take your time

Walking was our favourite way to discover George Town. Leave room in the day for detours, street art, old buildings, temples and unexpected discoveries.

A CHANGE OF SCENE

Penang Hill

Set aside time to head up into the hills. The views back over the city are fantastic, and keep an eye out for monkeys along the walking routes.

FOOD & DINING

Make time for dinner

We had some of our favourite experiences around the table. Peninsula House and Community Table are two evenings we still remember.

HOW LONG TO STAY

Allow a few days

Give yourself enough time to explore George Town properly, enjoy the restaurants and make a separate trip up to Penang Hill.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Culture, food and exploring

Penang is a lovely choice for travellers who enjoy historic streets, architecture, good restaurants and discovering a place at their own pace.

OUR EDIT

A city to explore slowly, and savour properly.

What we loved most about Penang was the variety of the experience. We could spend a day wandering through George Town, discovering old shophouses, street art and temples, then return to the Blue Mansion for a drink at the bar.

Penang Hill gave us a completely different perspective. The views back across the city were superb, and seeing monkeys while walking through the hills made the day even more memorable.

But the food was every bit as important. Peninsula House had just the right mix of relaxed and elegant, and Community Table gave us an evening that continued upstairs over wine.

For us, Penang was a lovely combination of character, culture, good food and the simple pleasure of taking your time. We'd happily go back.
