MALAYSIA

Penang

Walk, eat & discover George Town.

OUR PENANG

Walk, eat, discover.

We loved walking around Penang. George Town is the sort of place that rewards you for taking your time, with beautiful streets, old shophouses, street art and something interesting appearing around almost every corner.

We stayed at The Blue Mansion, which gave us a great base for exploring the city. But it was really the combination of wandering, eating and discovering that made Penang work so well for us.

We also spent a day up on Penang Hill. The views over the whole city were fantastic, but the wildlife made the experience even more memorable — we saw monkeys while walking through the hills.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Penang, as we saw it.

GEORGE TOWN

A city made for wandering.

Our favourite way to see George Town was simply on foot. We would head out with no particular plan and let the streets decide where we went next.

The old shophouse buildings were gorgeous, with their colours, details and layers of history giving the city a character that is difficult to reproduce anywhere else.

STREET ART

Always look up, around and behind you.

One of the fun things about walking George Town was spotting the street art. It never felt like we were following a checklist — there was always another piece appearing as we wandered through the city.

THE OLD CITY

Beautiful details everywhere.

Temples, old shopfronts, colourful facades and tiny details make George Town incredibly photogenic. The pleasure is in moving slowly enough to notice them.

TEMPLES & LANDMARKS

History around every corner.

Some of the temples we came across were beautiful and added another layer to the city. Then there are the landmarks that become part of the Penang experience — including the famous fire station.

WHERE WE STAYED

The Blue Mansion.

Cheong Fatt Tze — The Blue Mansion gave us a memorable base in George Town and fitted perfectly with the character of the city around it.

It was a lovely place to return to after a day spent walking, eating and exploring.

UP IN THE HILLS

A different view of Penang.

Penang Hill was one of our favourite days. The views back over the whole city were fantastic and gave us a completely different perspective on George Town.

And then there were the monkeys. Seeing them while walking through the hills made the day feel much more like an adventure than a sightseeing trip.

OUR FAVOURITE DAY

Penang Hill

On foot

OUR EDIT

Leave plenty of time to wander.

What we loved most about Penang was how easy it was to spend a day without much of a plan. We would walk through George Town, spot street art, admire the old shophouses and discover another temple or landmark along the way.

The city is incredibly photogenic. The old buildings, colourful details and famous fire station all became part of the fun of simply seeing what was around the next corner.

Food was another big part of the experience. Peninsula House was a particular highlight, while lunch at the Eastern & Oriental added another memorable stop.

And then there was Penang Hill. The views back over the whole city were superb, but seeing monkeys while walking through the hills made the day even more memorable.
