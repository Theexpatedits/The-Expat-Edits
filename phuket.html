JOURNEY 06

Phuket

Island days, blue water and nights that run late. From boat days across the Andaman Sea to long afternoons by the beach, Phuket gave us exactly the kind of escape we were looking for.

THE EXPERIENCE

Phuket, our way

Phuket is easy to think you already know. But once you get away from the traffic and out onto the water, the island feels very different.

We based ourselves at the InterContinental and settled quickly into the rhythm of beach mornings, boat days and evenings that carried on long after sunset.

The best days were spent exploring the nearby islands by boat — clear water, dramatic coastlines and the freedom to stop wherever looked too good to pass.

01

Out on the water

The best way to see Phuket is to leave it behind for a day. We spent our time exploring the nearby islands, moving between clear water, rocky coastlines and quiet stretches of beach.

There is something about being on a boat that makes the whole day slow down. No traffic, no timetable — just the sea and somewhere new to explore.

02

The islands

Phuket's real magic is scattered offshore. Dramatic limestone, turquoise water and small islands appear around almost every bend.

A boat day gives you the freedom to see the places that are impossible to reach from the road — and some of the best memories came from simply finding somewhere beautiful and stopping there.

03

Beach clubs & late nights

Back on the island, Phuket has another side. Afternoons at the beach clubs gradually become evenings, with Café del Mar providing the soundtrack for some of our later nights.

It is an easy rhythm: sun, sea, music and nowhere you particularly need to be. Some nights were about going out, others simply about letting the evening unfold.

04

Time to slow down

For all the energy around Phuket, some of our favourite moments were the quiet ones.

A coffee, an empty stretch of sand, the sea in front of us and a few hours with nowhere to go. Phuket has plenty going on, but you don't need to chase all of it.

DON'T MISS

The Phuket Edit

Boat days

Leave the island behind and spend a day exploring the surrounding islands by boat. The water is where Phuket really comes alive.

Café del Mar

Head to Café del Mar for the transition from late-afternoon beach time into music, drinks and a night that doesn't need to end early.

InterContinental Phuket

Our base for the trip and an easy place to settle into the west-coast rhythm of beach mornings, boat adventures and evenings out.

OUR TAKE

What we'd remember

Phuket works best when you do not try to do too much. Spend a day on the water, find a good beach, let an afternoon run into evening and keep a few hours completely free.

The island has plenty going on, but the memories are usually made in the spaces between the plans.
