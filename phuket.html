THAILAND

Phuket

Beach days, boat trips & nights of music.

OUR PHUKET

A little bit of everything. And always a reason to return.

Phuket has become one of our favourite regular escapes from Singapore. It is close enough for a weekend away, but once we arrive there is always plenty to fill the days. Good hotels, great restaurants, beautiful beaches and an increasingly exciting music scene.

We particularly enjoy staying around Kamala, where the InterContinental gives us a fantastic base on the beach. Café del Mar is just nearby, and we have increasingly found ourselves planning weekends around the DJs playing there.

But Phuket is not just about the evenings. We love hiring a boat with friends and family, exploring the islands and caves, swimming in the clear water and enjoying long afternoons on the beach. Add Bang Tao sunsets and some excellent places to eat, and it is easy to see why we keep coming back.

THE STAY

InterContinental Phuket Resort.

Our preferred base around Kamala, and a location that makes a Phuket weekend particularly easy to enjoy.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A superb location on the beach.

The InterContinental is a hotel we particularly enjoy. Its position on Kamala Beach is a big part of the appeal. It gives us a lovely place to stay while keeping us close to some of the best spots to eat, drink and spend time around this part of Phuket.

We like the fact that we can enjoy a relaxed morning, have a coffee by the beach and then decide how the day will unfold. There is no need to travel far to find somewhere good for lunch or a drink.

Café del Mar is close by, which is another reason the location works so well for us. We can enjoy the beach during the day and then head out for music and an evening with friends.

For anyone visiting Phuket who wants a comfortable hotel with a great west-coast location, this is somewhere we would certainly recommend considering.

For us, the combination of the beach, the nearby restaurants and the atmosphere around Kamala makes this a particularly good base for a weekend in Phuket.

THE LOCATION

Kamala, with everything close at hand.

One of the best things about staying here is the choice nearby. You have the beach, places to eat, beach bars and Café del Mar all within easy reach.

It is a location that lets you enjoy Phuket at your own pace, whether that means a quiet morning or a much livelier evening.

THE MUSIC

Phuket is becoming the Ibiza of Asia.

One of the reasons we love coming to Phuket for weekends is the growing music scene. There are some fantastic international acts coming through, and it has become a real part of the island's appeal for us.

KAMALA NIGHTS

Beach afternoons that turn into nights out.

Café del Mar is one of our favourite places to spend time when we are in Phuket. It is right by Kamala Beach and has become a familiar part of our weekends on the island.

We particularly enjoy travelling over when there is a DJ we want to see. Phuket is attracting more and more international names, and the quality of the acts coming through is making the island increasingly exciting for anyone who enjoys electronic music.

It is a different sort of weekend from Singapore. You can spend the afternoon by the sea, enjoy a good dinner and then find yourself dancing well into the evening.

For us, that combination is a big part of what makes Phuket so appealing.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering Phuket.

LIFE ON THE WATER

Our favourite way to explore.

We love hiring a boat when we are in Phuket, particularly when we have friends and family with us. It gives everyone the freedom to explore the coastline, find quieter spots and spend the day swimming.

Some of our favourite memories have been made away from the main beaches.

THE CAVES

A different side of the Andaman Sea.

Getting out on the water opens up a completely different side of the region. We have enjoyed exploring the cave areas, dramatic limestone landscapes and beautiful stretches of water.

Being able to stop for a swim in that clear blue-green sea is one of the real highlights.

THE ISLANDS

Somewhere to slow down.

There is something special about finding a quiet stretch of beach away from the busier parts of the island.

A boat day gives you the chance to enjoy the scenery, swim and spend time together without having to follow a fixed plan.

BANG TAO

Sunsets worth staying for.

We also enjoy spending time around Bang Tao. It has a different feel from Kamala, with good restaurants, beach spots and a relaxed atmosphere.

The sunsets here are particularly lovely. Image 12 captures exactly why it is worth staying on the beach as the day comes to an end.

FOOD & EXPERIENCES

Good food, good company and a few favourites.

THE DINING SCENE

Make time to explore beyond the hotel.

Phuket has some excellent places to eat. We enjoy trying different restaurants when we visit, from Thai food with a view to relaxed lunches by the sea.

OUR RESTAURANT PICKS

Baan Rim Pa.

Baan Rim Pa is a restaurant we would recommend to anyone visiting Phuket. Set high on the cliff, it offers wonderful views across the sea and is a particularly lovely place to enjoy Thai food as the sun goes down.

It is the sort of setting that makes dinner feel like part of the experience rather than simply somewhere to eat.

A LONG LUNCH

Rosewood Phuket.

We also enjoy heading to the Rosewood for lunch. It is a lovely setting and somewhere we like taking friends when they visit Phuket.

It makes for a particularly enjoyable afternoon, whether you are staying there or simply visiting for a meal.

PLANNING YOUR VISIT

How to make the most of Phuket.

Phuket works particularly well as a short break, but there is plenty to do if you allow yourself a little more time. These are a few things we would consider when planning a visit.

WHERE TO STAY

Kamala Beach

We particularly like the InterContinental. The location makes it easy to enjoy the beach, restaurants and nearby beach clubs without spending too much time travelling around.

OTHER AREAS

Bang Tao

Bang Tao offers a different atmosphere, with a good choice of restaurants, beach spots and beautiful sunsets. It is well worth spending time here.

BEST TIME TO VISIT

November – April

The dry season generally offers the most reliable beach weather, with warmer, sunnier days and more favourable conditions for getting out on the water.

GETTING THERE

Easy from Singapore

One of Phuket's biggest advantages for us is how easy it is to reach from Singapore. It is a very practical destination for a long weekend.

GETTING AROUND

By car and by boat

A car or driver makes it easier to explore different parts of the island. For the coastline and surrounding islands, hiring a boat is a highlight.

WHO WILL LOVE IT

Families, friends and couples

Phuket has a lot to offer different types of travellers. From family boat days and beach time to restaurants, hotels and music, there is plenty to make a trip your own.
