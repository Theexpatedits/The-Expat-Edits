Phuket

Island days, blue water and nights that run late.

The Journey

Phuket, our way

Phuket is easy to think you already know. But once you get away from the traffic and out onto the water, the island feels very different.

We based ourselves at the InterContinental and settled quickly into the rhythm of beach mornings, boat days and evenings that carried on long after sunset. Exploring the nearby islands by boat was the highlight — clear water, dramatic coastlines and the freedom to stop wherever looked too good to pass.

Back on the island, the mood changed. Café del Mar brought the music and late afternoons turned into nights, while some of the quieter moments were often the ones we remembered most.

01

Out on the water

The best way to see Phuket is to leave it behind for a day. We spent our time exploring the nearby islands, moving between clear water, rocky coastlines and quiet stretches of beach.

There is something about being on a boat that makes the whole day slow down. The scenery keeps changing, but there is never any real need to hurry.

02

The islands

Phuket's real magic is scattered offshore. Dramatic limestone, turquoise water and small islands appear around almost every bend.

A boat day gives you the freedom to see the places that are impossible to reach from the road and makes the island feel much bigger than it first appears.

03

Beach clubs & late nights

Back on the island, Phuket has another side. Afternoons at the beach clubs gradually become evenings, with Café del Mar providing the soundtrack for some of our later nights.

It is an easy rhythm: sun, sea, music and nowhere you particularly need to be.

04

Time to slow down

For all the energy around Phuket, some of our favourite moments were the quiet ones.

A coffee, an empty stretch of sand, the sea in front of us and a few hours with nowhere to go. Phuket works particularly well when you leave some space in the day.

From the camera

Phuket

Where we stayed

InterContinental Phuket

We based ourselves at the InterContinental Phuket and settled quickly into the rhythm of beach mornings, days out on the water and evenings around the west coast.

The location made it easy to move between slow beach days, boat adventures and the livelier side of Phuket without making the whole trip feel like a schedule.

Eat & Drink

Café del Mar

Café del Mar became part of the Phuket rhythm for us, particularly as afternoons started to turn into evenings.

It is the kind of place that works best when you do not have anywhere else you need to be — music, drinks, sunset and the sea providing an easy backdrop to the later part of the day.

Don't Miss

The Phuket experience

01

Get out on the water

The best way to experience Phuket is to spend a day among the islands. Leave the roads behind and see the coastline from the sea.

02

Let the day run late

Phuket is at its best when a beach afternoon does not have a fixed ending. Stay for sunset and let the evening happen naturally.

03

Leave time to do nothing

There is plenty to see, but some of the best memories come from the spaces between the plans — a quiet beach, a coffee or a few hours beside the sea.

Our Take

Phuket is better when you slow it down.

Phuket has plenty going on, but for us the best version of the island was a balance between the two sides of it. A day on the water, a good beach, an afternoon that becomes evening and a few hours with nowhere to be. That was the rhythm that made the trip work.
