Phuket

Beaches, boats, music and an easy escape from Singapore.

OUR PHUKET

Our favourite kind of weekend escape.

We go to Phuket frequently. From Singapore it is remarkably easy to get to, which makes it one of those places we can return to without needing to turn it into a major trip.

We tend to base ourselves around Kamala, usually at the InterContinental, where being so close to Café del Mar makes it particularly easy to move from beach days into long afternoons and nights of music.

But Phuket is much more than the beach in front of the hotel. We love getting out on the water, exploring the national park, finding quieter stretches of coastline, eating well and spending time around Bang Tao too.

THE ESCAPE

Easy to reach. Easy to love.

One of the reasons Phuket keeps pulling us back is simply how easy it is from Singapore. A short flight can take us from city life to the Andaman Sea, without sacrificing good hotels, restaurants or things to do once we arrive.

KAMALA

Our usual base.

We tend to stay at the InterContinental. Its position on Kamala Beach works perfectly for us, particularly because Café del Mar is right next door.

THE BEACH CLUBS

From afternoon into night.

Phuket's beach-club scene has become a big part of its appeal. Café del Mar gives Kamala an easy place to settle in as the sun goes down.

MUSIC

The Ibiza of Asia?

Phuket is increasingly becoming one of Asia's big destinations for electronic music. More and more international DJs are coming through, and we love the fact that a beach weekend can turn into a very good night out.

BANG TAO

A different side of Phuket.

We also enjoy spending time around Bang Tao. There is a slightly different rhythm here, with good restaurants, beach spots and plenty of reasons to stay for the afternoon.

WHERE WE STAY

InterContinental

Our usual base around Kamala. We particularly like the location because Café del Mar is next door and the beach is right outside.

OTHER HOTELS

Rosewood & Cape Sienna

We have also stayed at the Rosewood and Cape Sienna. Both give you a different perspective on the west coast and are worth knowing when planning a Phuket stay.

THE OTHER SIDE

By boat

Some of our favourite Phuket days are spent away from the island itself, exploring the national park, caves, coastline and clear water by boat.

OUR EDIT

Phuket works because it never needs to be just one thing.

We come back to Phuket because it gives us the combination we want from a short break. There is the beach, the food, the hotels and enough going on at night to make the trip feel like more than simply lying by a pool.

Kamala is our usual base, largely because the InterContinental puts us directly beside the beach and Café del Mar. We like being able to walk from a relaxed afternoon into an evening of music, and Phuket is increasingly attracting some of the biggest DJs in Asia.

But we also like to escape the developed parts of the island. A boat day into the national park gives you caves, islands, coastline and exceptionally clear water. Add Bang Tao, some very good food and the fact that Singapore is only a short flight away, and it is easy to understand why Phuket has become one of our regular escapes.

THAILAND

Phuket

Beaches, boat days and nights around Kamala.

OUR PHUKET

The best of Phuket is by the sea.

We based ourselves on Kamala Beach at the InterContinental and quickly settled into the rhythm of beach mornings, boat days and evenings that could carry on long after sunset. For us, it was the perfect base for exploring this side of Phuket.

The InterContinental sits right next to Café del Mar and some other great beach bars, so there was always somewhere nearby to head for as the day started to wind down. It made the location feel particularly easy — the beach on one side and plenty happening just beyond it.

We also took a boat out for the day and headed into the national park, exploring some fantastic cave areas and stopping to swim in incredibly clear, crystal-blue seas. Getting out onto the water was one of the highlights of the trip.

We spent time on Bang Tao Beach too, where there are some good restaurants and a slightly different feel from Kamala. Another hotel worth knowing is Cape Sienna, but for us the InterContinental is the best place to stay if you want to explore the beaches and beach bars around this part of Phuket.

OUR EDIT

Stay in Kamala and explore from there.

For us, the InterContinental was the right base. Being directly on Kamala Beach and next to Café del Mar meant that we could keep the days relaxed without being cut off from everything happening around the beach.

The real highlight, though, was getting away from the island for a day. Heading into the national park by boat, finding the cave areas and jumping into that incredibly clear water was exactly the sort of experience we wanted from Phuket.

Add in a day at Bang Tao Beach, some good restaurants and evenings around Kamala, and the whole trip had a very easy rhythm. For us, it is the best way to do Phuket.
