OUR HOME

Singapore

A different perspective on living in the city.

OUR SINGAPORE

More than a place to live.

Singapore has become much more than the place we moved to. After spending our lives living and working in the UK, relocating here gave our family a new lease on life. The pace, the weather, the safety and the opportunities have made the transition surprisingly effortless.

Our children are growing up here, and what began as a move abroad has become home. We love the food, the bars, the city and the everyday rhythm of life. Condo living, afternoons by the pool and weekends on Sentosa have become part of our routine.

Perhaps the biggest change has been how much Singapore has reignited our love of travel. With Changi on our doorstep, Asia feels wonderfully accessible. Friends and family now have another reason to visit, and we have a fantastic base from which to explore.

THE STAYS

Two hotels that feel part of our Singapore life.

We don't always think of hotels as somewhere to stay. Sometimes they are simply places to spend an afternoon, enjoy a drink, meet friends or change the pace of a familiar weekend. These are two that we particularly enjoy.

01 / GRAND HYATT

Lazy afternoons by the pool.

The Grand Hyatt is one of our favourite places to spend a relaxed afternoon in Singapore. Its pool area is lovely, and we often find ourselves settling in for a long lunch, a few drinks and very little else.

The rooms are great, and the gym is particularly well appointed. It is a hotel that works just as well for a proper stay as it does for enjoying a few hours away from the usual routine.

For us, the appeal is that it feels easy. No big plans, no need to go anywhere else: just a good pool, something to eat and drink, and a lazy afternoon.

A favourite for poolside afternoons, relaxed food and drinks, and a little time to unwind in the middle of the city.

02 / THE SINGAPORE EDITION

A different kind of afternoon and evening.

The Singapore EDITION has a completely different feel. We often spend time in the lobby bar, where the pink marble pool table has become something of a favourite feature.

It is a lovely place to meet friends, enjoy a drink and ease into an evening. And quite often, the evening doesn't end there. We regularly find ourselves heading downstairs to the nightclub.

It is the sort of hotel that gives you a reason to stay out a little longer. Stylish without feeling too formal, and with enough going on to make it an enjoyable part of a night in the city.

Between the relaxed afternoons at the Grand Hyatt and the social atmosphere of the EDITION, we have two very different ways of enjoying Singapore hospitality.

OUR CITY

A familiar skyline that never gets old.

One of the things we still enjoy about Singapore is how the city changes with the light, the weather and the neighbourhood. Even after living here for some time, there is always another view worth stopping for.

FROM OUR CAMERA

Singapore, as we see it.

THE CITY

Modern, green and full of contrast.

Singapore's skyline is unmistakable, but what makes the city interesting is the way it sits alongside tropical greenery. Towers rise behind flowering trees, busy roads disappear into leafy streets, and the urban landscape constantly gives way to something more natural.

It is a city that feels energetic without being overwhelming.

ARCHITECTURE

A city that keeps looking forward.

The architecture is one of the things we never tire of. From the instantly recognisable structures around the city to the striking buildings that appear in everyday neighbourhoods, there is always something interesting to look up at.

GREEN SINGAPORE

Nature is part of everyday life.

Singapore's greenery is one of its greatest pleasures. Trees, gardens and landscaped spaces soften the city, and the tropical environment is never far away.

It is something you notice more the longer you live here.

A CHANGE OF PACE

The coast is never far away.

One of the pleasures of living here is being able to change the scenery without making a major journey. Sometimes a weekend simply calls for a beach, a sunset and a slower afternoon.

Sentosa is an easy escape from the city, and a reminder that Singapore has more than one side.

THE UNEXPECTED

Wildlife is part of the neighbourhood.

One of the things that still makes us smile is the wildlife. Monitor lizards, otters and snakes appear in places you might not expect, and tropical nature is never very far from everyday life.

It is a reminder that this is a very different environment from the one we left behind in the UK.

FOOD & CULTURE

A city with its own character.

Food is one of the great pleasures of living in Singapore. The city brings together so many different cultures, flavours and traditions, and there is always something new to discover.

From everyday local food to the more unusual things you come across, it is part of what makes life here so interesting.

AFTER DARK

The city takes on another life.

Singapore is a different place after dark. The waterfront lights up, the restaurants and bars fill, and the skyline becomes part of the evening.

It is one of the things we enjoy most about living here: there is always somewhere to go, but equally, there is no pressure to make every evening an occasion.

CULTURAL DETAIL

Another side of the city.

Singapore's character comes from its many communities and traditions. Even after dark, the architecture and heritage of the city provide a different perspective from the familiar modern skyline.

THE DETAILS

More moments from home.

EVERYDAY SINGAPORE

The little things matter too.

From the architecture overhead to the food, gardens and unexpected details along the way, these are the moments that make up our everyday experience of the city.

LIVING IN SINGAPORE

A few things we've come to appreciate.

Living here is quite different from visiting for a few days. These are some of the things that have shaped our experience of making Singapore home.

THE LIFESTYLE

Outdoor living

The warm weather makes it easy to spend time outside. Pools, gardens, waterfronts and outdoor dining have become a natural part of our routine.

THE LOCATION

Asia on our doorstep

Changi makes travelling around Asia remarkably convenient. Weekend breaks and longer adventures are much easier to plan when so many destinations are within reach.

EVERYDAY LIFE

Comfort and convenience

The combination of safety, excellent transport, food, shopping and an efficient city infrastructure has made settling into life here surprisingly straightforward.

THE WEATHER

Tropical year round

Singapore is warm and humid throughout the year, with regular rainfall. The weather is something you learn to work around, and we have come to enjoy the tropical environment.

GETTING AROUND

Easy to navigate

A compact city, excellent public transport and readily available taxis make getting around straightforward. Changi is also a major advantage for anyone who enjoys travelling.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A great place for families

For our family, Singapore has provided a wonderful environment in which to live, work, explore and grow. It has become much more than simply an overseas posting.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places we love.

01

Grand Hyatt

Our favourite for lazy poolside afternoons, good food and drinks. The rooms are great and the gym is well appointed.

02

The Singapore EDITION

A stylish social spot. We love the pink marble pool table in the lobby bar and often end up downstairs in the nightclub.

03

Raffles

A Singapore classic. We particularly enjoy the courtyard, a peaceful setting in the middle of the city and somewhere that captures a little of Singapore's history.

04

Sentosa

An easy weekend escape. When we want a change of scenery, the beaches and relaxed atmosphere make Sentosa a natural choice.

05

The food scene

From hawker centres to some of Asia's best restaurants, eating is one of the great pleasures of living here. It is something we never tire of.

06

Gardens by the Bay

A remarkable example of Singapore's relationship with architecture and nature. The Cloud Forest is particularly impressive.

OUR EDIT

Singapore has become home.

When we first moved here, Singapore was somewhere new to discover. Over time it has become the place we return to after travelling, the backdrop to family life and the starting point for so many adventures around Asia.

We still enjoy exploring the city, trying new restaurants, spending afternoons at the hotels we love and finding little details we hadn't noticed before. But perhaps the greatest change is that we no longer look at Singapore as somewhere we are visiting. It is simply home.

Moving abroad was a big decision, but looking back, it has given our family an experience and a lifestyle that we are very grateful for.
