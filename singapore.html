OUR HOME

Singapore

Life, travel & everything in between.

The Experience

Our Singapore

Singapore is different for us because it isn't simply somewhere we have travelled to. It is home.

Since moving to Singapore, we have discovered a side of the city that goes far beyond its famous skyline. Tropical greenery is everywhere, wildlife appears in the most unexpected places and the waterfront becomes part of everyday life.

What we love most is the contrast. One moment we're surrounded by skyscrapers and the energy of the city, and the next we're watching monitor lizards by the water, discovering a snake in the trees or picking bananas that have grown in our own garden.

Singapore has also become the base for so much of our travelling around Asia. We come home here between trips, which has made the city part of the wider story behind The Expat Edits.

01

The city

Singapore's skyline is one of the most recognisable in Asia, but living here changes the way you see it. The towers, waterfront and spectacular architecture become part of the landscape of everyday life rather than simply something to photograph.

That mixture of city and nature is one of the things we enjoy most about living here.

02

Life by the water

Some of our favourite Singapore moments are the ones that simply happen here.

Watching our son DJ by the waterfront is one of them — a reminder that this isn't just somewhere we visit, but somewhere our family is building its own memories.

03

Our tropical garden

One of the things we love about Singapore is how easily tropical life becomes part of everyday life.

These bananas grow in our own garden, which still feels like a pretty special thing to say about the place we now call home.

Places We Know

Singapore hotels

Because Singapore is home, the hotels here are less about where we need to stay and more about the places that have become part of our Singapore life.

The Edition is one of those places we particularly enjoy, while the Grand Hyatt has provided a great excuse for a poolside drink and a slower afternoon.

And then there is Raffles — somewhere that feels unmistakably Singapore, with its beautiful courtyard and sense of history.

Hotel

The Edition

The Edition is one of the Singapore hotels that has become part of our local rotation.

It feels less like somewhere we need to visit and more like one of those places that fits naturally into life in the city.

Poolside

Grand Hyatt

Sometimes the best Singapore experience is doing very little at all.

A poolside drink at the Grand Hyatt is exactly that — somewhere to slow down for an afternoon and enjoy the fact that this is home.

Singapore Classic

Raffles

Raffles is one of those Singapore places that needs very little introduction.

We particularly enjoy the courtyard — a remarkably peaceful setting in the middle of the city and one that captures something of the Singapore we love.

Our Take

Home, but never boring.

Singapore has become the base from which so much of our travelling begins. But the longer we live here, the more we appreciate the place itself — the skyline, the waterfront, the hotels, the tropical garden and the unexpected wildlife that somehow becomes part of normal life.

Most importantly, this is where our family is making memories. From watching our son DJ by the water to picking bananas in the garden, these are the moments that make Singapore home.
