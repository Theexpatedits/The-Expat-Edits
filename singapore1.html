THE EXPAT EDITS · SINGAPORE

Singapore

Home, somewhere in Asia.

OUR HOME

A new lease on life.

Singapore has been home for us for several years. It is where we work, where the children are growing up, where we run, eat, explore and spend our weekends — and where Changi makes the rest of Asia feel remarkably close.

After spending our lives living and working in the UK, moving here changed the rhythm of our family life. Singapore has become much more than the place we live. It has become the starting point for almost everything we do.

THE CITY

A city that rewards looking up.

We spend a lot of time around the city during the week. Work takes us there, lunch often becomes a run, and there is always another building, street or colonial corner worth stopping to look at.

Singapore's mix of old and new is one of the things we enjoy most about living here. Glass towers, old shophouses, colonial buildings and tropical greenery all sit remarkably close together.

This is the everyday Singapore for us — not a checklist of sights, but the city we pass through on the way to lunch, dinner or a drink.

LIFE BY THE WATER

When the city lights up.

Around Marina Bay there are plenty of places we enjoy settling into for an evening.

But some of our favourite Singapore memories aren't about the skyline at all. They are the moments that happen around it — friends visiting, evenings by the water and watching the children find their own way into the life of the city.

Our son's DJing at Marina Bay Sands has given him confidence and created some genuinely magical memories. Those are the moments that make Singapore feel like home rather than simply somewhere we happen to live.

PLACES WE RETURN TO

The hotels have become part of home.

01

Raffles

The courtyard is one of our favourite places to slow down in the middle of the city. Colonial Singapore, drinks and an atmosphere that never feels rushed.

02

The Singapore Edition

Snacks by the pool, a swim and a game of pool in the bar. It has become somewhere we enjoy spending an afternoon rather than somewhere we simply stay.

03

Hyatt on Scott's

The pool bar is relaxed and easy — one of those places where you can settle in for an afternoon without needing much of a plan.

FOOD & NEIGHBOURHOODS

Dinner is rarely far away.

We most frequently find ourselves around Duxton Hill and Amoy Street. There are so many bars and restaurants within a small area that choosing where to eat can sometimes be the hardest part.

Chinatown and the surrounding streets are also some of our favourite places to wander in the evening, particularly when friends or family are visiting.

GREEN SINGAPORE

The jungle is never very far away.

One of the things we appreciate most about living here is how easy it is to escape the buildings.

We love exploring MacRitchie Reservoir, the Botanic Gardens and Fort Canning Park, particularly for the wildlife. Singapore can feel intensely urban one minute and surprisingly wild the next.

FAMILY LIFE

The experiences outside the travel.

Living here has given the children so many experiences that have nothing to do with getting on a plane.

And then there are the smaller things — tropical fruit, wildlife in the parks, weekends around the pool and the simple freedom to explore.

HOME GROWN

Even our garden feels tropical.

We are lucky enough to grow bananas in our garden, and living in Asia has completely changed our relationship with fruit.

There is always something new to try, from familiar favourites to the more exotic fruit that appears in markets around Singapore.

WEEKENDS

Down to Sentosa before sunset.

Weekends are often an excuse to get down to Sentosa with the kids.

Our favourite time is just before sunset. Settle into a drink at Tanjong Beach Club while the heat comes out of the day, then walk along the beach and find somewhere relaxed for something to eat.

Changi

One of the great luxuries of living here is how easy weekends away can be. Changi makes the rest of Asia feel remarkably accessible.

Dempsey Hill

Another favourite for eating and lounging. It has enough of a change of pace to feel like an outing without requiring much effort.

Chinatown

We love walking around in the evening, particularly when friends and family are visiting.

OUR SINGAPORE

More than a place to live.

The longer we live here, the more Singapore feels like a collection of small routines rather than a destination.

A run through the city. Lunch somewhere new. A drink at Raffles. Pool time at the Hyatt. Dinner around Duxton Hill. A sunset on Sentosa. Wildlife at MacRitchie. Then, when the mood takes us, Changi and somewhere completely different.

That is our Singapore.
