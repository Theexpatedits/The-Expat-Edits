Singapore.

Our home in Asia. A city of contrasts, where modern architecture, tropical greenery and neighbourhood life sit remarkably close together.

Singapore · Home Edition
01

The City

A city of contrasts, where a glittering skyline, tropical greenery and neighbourhood life all sit remarkably close together. It is modern, but it rarely feels detached from the natural world.

City life, redefined.

Singapore has the energy of a major Asian city, but the scale is different. The centre can feel dense and dramatic one moment, then quiet and green only a short distance away.

02

Green Spaces

Greenery is part of everyday life here. Parks, gardens and waterfront spaces give the city room to breathe — and make it surprisingly easy to slow down when you want to.

03

The Neighbourhoods

Heritage streets, shopping districts, waterfront towers and high-rise living all sit within a short journey of one another. Singapore constantly changes character depending on where you are.

Different sides of the city.

This is one of the things we enjoy most about Singapore. A short taxi ride can completely change the atmosphere around you.

04

After Dark

The waterfront, restaurants and skyline all feel different once the sun goes down. Some evenings are about dinner and drinks; others are simply about being outside and watching the city change.

Singapore after sunset.

The heat softens, the lights come on and the waterfront becomes one of the best places to simply spend an evening.

05

The Unexpected

Even after years here, Singapore still surprises us. Wildlife appears where you least expect it, tropical life continues around the edges of the city, and there always seems to be another detail worth stopping for.

06

Our Singapore

Getting Around

Easy by design.

The MRT is efficient and taxis are plentiful, but some of our favourite journeys are simply walks through the neighbourhoods.

The Weather

Tropical, always.

Expect heat, humidity and sudden rain. The weather is part of the rhythm of life here.

Our Advice

Slow down.

Singapore is easy to explore, but it rewards spending time in one neighbourhood rather than trying to see everything in a day.

07

Places We Return To

Hotel

The Edition

Hotel

08

Journal

Living in Singapore changes the way you experience it. The famous skyline becomes part of everyday life, favourite restaurants become neighbourhood habits, and the places that matter most are often the ones you return to without thinking.
