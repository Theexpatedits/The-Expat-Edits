Singapore

Life, travel & everything in between.

OUR SINGAPORE

A new lease on life.

Living in Singapore has given our family a new lease on life. After spending our lives living and working in the UK, the combination of an easier pace, great weather and an incredible sense of safety has made the move surprisingly effortless — and our kids are growing and thriving here. Singapore has also reignited our love of travel. With Changi on our doorstep, weekends can mean a pool day or Sentosa, while longer breaks are just a flight away. We love the food, the bars, the city and condo life — but perhaps most of all, we love that friends and family now have a reason to come and see us.

Singapore, as we see it.

THE CITY

Singapore, as we see it.

A city of contrasts, where lush greenery sits alongside a glittering skyline, and every neighbourhood has its own story to tell.

GREEN SPACES

The city never feels far from nature.

From the Botanic Gardens to the parks and waterfront, there is always somewhere to slow down.

THE NEIGHBOURHOODS

Different sides of the city.

From heritage streets to high-rise living, Singapore constantly changes character.

AFTER DARK

When the city lights up.

The waterfront, the restaurants and the skyline all feel different once the sun goes down.

THE UNEXPECTED

There is always something else to notice.

Even after years here, Singapore still surprises us — sometimes in the most unexpected places.

BEST TIME

Year round

Singapore is warm and tropical throughout the year. We enjoy being here whenever we're not travelling.

WEATHER

Tropical

Expect heat, humidity and regular rain — usually followed by another beautiful stretch of weather.

GETTING AROUND

Effortless

A compact city, excellent transport and Changi Airport make Singapore an exceptionally easy base for exploring Asia.

OUR RECOMMENDATIONS

Places we love.

01

The Singapore Edition

One of the hotels that has become part of our local rotation. It feels like somewhere that fits naturally into life in the city rather than somewhere we need to make a special occasion of.

02

Grand Hyatt

Sometimes the best Singapore experience is doing very little at all. A poolside drink and a slow afternoon is one of our favourite ways to enjoy the city.

03

Raffles

A Singapore classic. We particularly enjoy the courtyard — a peaceful setting in the middle of the city and one of the places that captures something of Singapore's character.

04

Sentosa

When the weekend calls for something simple, Sentosa gives us an easy escape from the city without really leaving Singapore.

05

The food scene

From hawker centres to some of Asia's best restaurants, eating is one of the great pleasures of living here. It is one of the things we never tire of.

06

Life by the water

The waterfront is one of our favourite parts of Singapore — particularly when the city starts to slow down in the evening.

FROM THE JOURNAL

Living in Singapore.

The longer we live here, the more there is to notice. From family life and condo weekends to the food, bars, beaches and unexpected wildlife, Singapore has become much more than the place we moved to.

A deeper look at what it is actually like to build a family life here will become part of our Journal.
