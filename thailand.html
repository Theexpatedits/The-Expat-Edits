THAILAND

Island Life

Three islands, hidden beaches and a slower rhythm.

THE EXPERIENCE

Three islands, one unforgettable escape.

There is something rather special about travelling between the islands in the Gulf of Thailand. We managed to experience three on this trip — Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao — and each offered something quite different.

We began in the north of Koh Samui, where a quiet beachfront hotel gave us a lovely base for exploring the island by car. From there, Koh Phangan became an adventure of boats, beaches and sunsets, before finishing in Koh Tao, where the pace slowed down once again.

Looking back, it was the contrast between the three islands that made the trip work so well. We wouldn't have wanted to choose just one.

WHERE WE STAYED

Three islands, three different bases.

Each island had its own character, and having a different base on each gave us the chance to experience them at their own pace.

KOH SAMUI — THE STAY

Sarei Samui.

We stayed in the north of Samui at Sarei Samui, and it turned out to be a lovely choice. The setting was peaceful, with the hotel sitting directly on a beautiful, quiet beach.

The pool was great, but one of our favourite parts of the stay was breakfast at the beachfront restaurant. It was a lovely way to start the day before heading out to explore the island.

The north worked particularly well as a base. It gave us the quieter side of Samui while keeping the rest of the island within easy reach by car.

KOH PHANGAN — THE STAY

Varivana.

Our base on Koh Phangan was Varivana. This was the island where we spent much of our time exploring, getting out on the water and discovering different stretches of coastline.

Koh Phangan is a superb island to explore. We found that arranging transport every day could become eye-wateringly expensive, so getting out by boat became a much more enjoyable way to discover the island.

KOH TAO — THE STAY

The Heritage Hideaway.

We finished the trip at The Heritage Hideaway on Koh Tao. After the exploring and boat adventures of the previous islands, this was a lovely place to settle into a slower rhythm.

Koh Tao was all about taking things a little easier, wandering the streets, spending time on the beaches and enjoying some well-earned downtime.

BEYOND THE HOTELS

Three islands, three different rhythms.

01 — KOH SAMUI

The quieter side of Samui.

The north of Koh Samui was exactly what we wanted at the start of the trip. Peaceful, relaxed and right on the beach, it gave us a chance to settle in before exploring further afield.

EXPLORING BY CAR

Freedom to find your own way.

We hired a car and loved having the freedom to explore at our own pace. It made discovering different parts of the island easy, with no need to plan every stop in advance.

FISHERMAN'S VILLAGE

Evenings by the water.

In the evenings we loved heading to Fisherman's Village. It was a great place to spend time eating, wandering around the shops and enjoying a different side of Samui after a day exploring.

02 — KOH PHANGAN

The island adventure.

Koh Phangan was probably the island we enjoyed exploring most. Getting around by boat allowed us to discover beaches that would otherwise have been much harder to reach.

BOTTLE BEACH & SECRET BEACH

One beach after another.

We loved Bottle Beach and Secret Beach. Simply bounding around the coast, stopping at different beaches and seeing what each one had to offer, was a joy.

MA ISLAND & AMSTERDAM BAR

A day on the water, then a remarkable sunset.

We also took a boat over to Ma Island. It was a fun day exploring another beautiful part of the coastline.

We found Amsterdam Bar and all agreed that it was possibly the best sunset bar in the world. The atmosphere, the music and the view made it one of those places that stays with you.

03 — KOH TAO

A slower finish.

By the time we reached Koh Tao, we were ready for a slightly slower pace. We enjoyed walking around the cobbled streets, discovering the island and spending time relaxing on the beaches.

It felt like a different world again, with a much more laid-back atmosphere and plenty of time to simply enjoy being there.
