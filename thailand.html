THAILAND

Island Life

Three islands, hidden beaches and a slower rhythm of life.

THE EXPERIENCE

Three islands,
one escape

We managed to cover three islands on this trip — Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao — and each had its own character.

We based ourselves in Choeng Mon in the north of Koh Samui, which made it easy to get over to Fisherman's Village. We also hired a car and spent time exploring the island, including a long lunch with friends at The Cliff.

From Samui we travelled by boat to Koh Phangan, where we used a mixture of cars and boats to get out to some spectacular hidden beaches, including Bottle Beach, Zen Beach and Secret Beach.

Koh Tao had a lovely laid-back feel. We enjoyed slowing things down, having massages by the beach and eating at Alcove Restaurant, which was possibly our highlight of the island.

06

Alcove Restaurant

Possibly our highlight of Koh Tao. A stunning restaurant and a meal that really stood out from the trip.

OUR EDIT

Don't just stay on one island.

What made this trip work so well was moving between the islands. Koh Samui gave us the easiest base for exploring, Koh Phangan gave us the adventure of finding hidden beaches, and Koh Tao slowed everything down again.

We loved being able to jump in a car on Samui and explore, then get on a boat and suddenly find ourselves somewhere completely different. The beaches on Koh Phangan were a particular highlight, especially Bottle Beach, Zen Beach and Secret Beach.

But Amsterdam Bar deserves a special mention. Sitting there with the music playing while the sun melted into the sea was one of those travel moments that stays with you.
