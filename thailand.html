THAILAND'S GULF ISLANDS

Koh Samui, Koh Phangan & Koh Tao

Three islands, turquoise water and the kind of tropical scenery that makes it very easy to slow down and stay a little longer.

Begin Journey

The Experience

Three islands, three different personalities

Thailand's Gulf Islands are the sort of place where days quickly lose their structure. Our time between Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao was a mixture of beautiful beaches, warm turquoise water, longtail boats, great food and plenty of reasons simply to stop and take it all in.

Koh Samui has the energy and variety, Koh Phangan brings a more laid-back island feel, while Koh Tao feels smaller and more intimate. Travelling between them makes the experience even better, with each island offering something slightly different.

For us, the real appeal was simply being by the water. Early mornings, quiet beaches, sunsets, jungle-covered hills and the occasional longtail boat passing through the frame are exactly the sort of moments that make island life so addictive.

01

Koh Samui

Koh Samui is the easiest place to settle into island life. There is plenty going on when you want it, but it is just as easy to disappear onto a beautiful stretch of sand and let the day unfold. Palm trees, warm water, colourful boats and spectacular sunsets make it an island that is hard not to love.

02

Koh Phangan

Away from the famous Full Moon parties, Koh Phangan has a wonderfully relaxed side. Jungle-covered hills meet quiet beaches and clear water, creating the feeling of a much more remote island. It is the sort of place where the best moments are often the simplest ones.

03

Koh Tao

Smaller and more intimate, Koh Tao feels like the perfect place to finish an island adventure. Its rocky coastline, hidden bays and impossibly clear water create some of the most beautiful scenery of the three islands. From the viewpoints to the beaches below, there is always another view waiting around the corner.

THE STARTING POINT

Koh Samui

Samui is the natural starting point for exploring Thailand's Gulf Islands. It has the widest choice of restaurants, beaches and places to stay, while still offering plenty of opportunities to escape somewhere quiet and beautiful.

THE ISLANDS

Koh Phangan & Koh Tao

The real joy comes from moving between the islands. Koh Phangan offers a more relaxed, jungle-covered landscape, while Koh Tao feels smaller and more intimate. Together they make a wonderful contrast to the energy and variety of Samui.
