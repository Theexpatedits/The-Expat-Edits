THAILAND

Island Life

Three islands, hidden beaches and a slower rhythm.

THE EXPERIENCE

Three islands,
one unforgettable escape.

There is something rather special about travelling between the islands in the Gulf of Thailand. We managed to experience three on this trip — Koh Samui, Koh Phangan and Koh Tao — and each offered something quite different.

We began in the north of Koh Samui, where a quiet beachfront hotel gave us a lovely base for exploring the island by car. From there, Koh Phangan became an adventure of boats, beaches and sunsets, before finishing in Koh Tao, where the pace slowed down once again.

Looking back, it was the contrast between the three islands that made the trip work so well. We wouldn't have wanted to choose just one.

WHERE WE STAYED

Three islands, three different bases.

KOH SAMUI

Sarei Samui

We stayed in the north of Samui at Sarei Samui, and it turned out to be a lovely choice. The setting was peaceful, with the hotel sitting directly on a beautiful, quiet beach.

The pool was great, but one of our favourite parts of the stay was breakfast at the beachfront restaurant. It was a lovely way to start the day before heading out to explore the island.

The north worked particularly well as a base. It gave us the quieter side of Samui while keeping the rest of the island within easy reach by car.

KOH PHANGAN

Varivana

Our base on Koh Phangan was Varivana. This was the island where we spent much of our time exploring, getting out on the water and discovering different stretches of coastline.

Koh Phangan is a superb island to explore. We found that arranging transport every day could become eye-wateringly expensive, so getting out by boat became a much more enjoyable way to discover the island.

KOH TAO

The Heritage Hideaway

We finished the trip at The Heritage Hideaway on Koh Tao. After the exploring and boat adventures of the previous islands, this was a lovely place to settle into a slower rhythm.

Koh Tao was all about taking things a little easier, wandering the streets, spending time on the beaches and enjoying some well-earned downtime.

01 — KOH SAMUI

The quieter side of Samui.

OUR FIRST STOP

A lovely place to begin.

The north of Koh Samui was exactly what we wanted at the start of the trip. Peaceful, relaxed and right on the beach, it gave us a chance to settle in before exploring further afield.

We hired a car and loved having the freedom to explore at our own pace. It made discovering different parts of the island easy, with no need to plan every stop in advance.

ISLAND EXPLORING

Beaches, roads and little discoveries.

We enjoyed getting out and seeing more of Samui. Having the car meant we could explore different beaches and corners of the island without having to think too much about getting around.

A QUIETER MOMENT

Finding our own little corners.

Some of the nicest moments were simply finding a quiet stretch of beach and enjoying the slower pace of island life.

AROUND THE ISLAND

Exploring at our own pace.

Samui is a good island to explore without rushing. We enjoyed the freedom of being able to head off for the day and see where the road took us.

EVENINGS

Fisherman's Village.

In the evenings we loved heading to Fisherman's Village. It was a great place to spend time eating, wandering around the shops and enjoying a different side of Samui after a day exploring.

02 — KOH PHANGAN

The island adventure.

EXPLORING BY BOAT

Get out on the water.

Koh Phangan was probably the island we enjoyed exploring most. The coastline is beautiful, and getting around by boat allowed us to discover beaches that would otherwise have been much harder to reach.

Taxis were eye-wateringly expensive every day, so exploring by boat made a lot of sense. More importantly, it was great fun.

THE COASTLINE

One beach after another.

We loved simply bounding around the coast, stopping at different beaches and seeing what each one had to offer. It was one of those days where the journey was just as enjoyable as the destinations.

BOTTLE BEACH

Worth making the effort.

Bottle Beach was one of the highlights. It is exactly the sort of place that makes exploring the island worthwhile.

SECRET BEACH

Finding somewhere quieter.

Secret Beach was another favourite. Discovering these different stretches of coastline was a big part of what made Koh Phangan so enjoyable.

MA ISLAND

A day out on the water.

We also took a boat over to Ma Island. It was a fun day exploring another beautiful part of the coastline and enjoying everything that makes this island group so appealing.

AMSTERDAM BAR

Possibly the best sunset bar in the world.

We found Amsterdam Bar and all agreed that it was possibly the best sunset bar in the world.

The atmosphere, the music and the view as the sun went down made it one of those places that stays with you long after the trip has finished.

THE SUNSET

One of those evenings.

There is something about watching the sun disappear over the water in the islands. Amsterdam Bar gave us the perfect setting to enjoy it.

DINNER

A table at Alcove.

For dinner, Alcove was another great find. It was a lovely spot to spend an evening after a day exploring the island.

ISLAND EVENINGS

The simple pleasures.

Boat trips, quiet beaches, good food and sunsets. Koh Phangan gave us some of the most memorable moments of the trip.

03 — KOH TAO

A slower finish.

KOH TAO

Time to slow down.

By the time we reached Koh Tao, we were ready for a slightly slower pace. We enjoyed walking around the cobbled streets, discovering the island and spending time relaxing on the beaches.

It felt like a different world again, with a much more laid-back atmosphere and plenty of time to simply enjoy being there.

BEACH DAYS

Nothing too complicated.

We spent time relaxing on the beaches and enjoying the slower life. After all the exploring on Samui and Phangan, it was a lovely way to finish the trip.

THE FINAL CHAPTER

A fitting end to the islands.

Koh Tao brought the pace right down. We enjoyed the beaches, the streets and the chance to relax before heading home.

THE THINGS WE LEARNED

A few thoughts before you go.

KOH SAMUI

Stay in the north if you want a quieter base.

We loved the peaceful feel of the north. Having a car made exploring the island straightforward, and Fisherman's Village was a great place to spend the evenings.

KOH PHANGAN

Explore the coastline by boat.

The beaches were a huge highlight. We found exploring by boat a much more enjoyable way to get around than relying on expensive taxis.

KOH TAO

Leave time to do very little.

Koh Tao was a lovely place to slow down. Enjoy the beaches, wander around the streets and take the opportunity to relax.

MORE FROM OUR CAMERA

Moments from the islands.

OUR EDIT

Don't just stay on one island.

If we were planning this trip again, we wouldn't change the idea of visiting all three islands.

Samui gave us the peaceful beachfront base and the freedom to explore by car. Koh Phangan brought the adventure: boats, hidden beaches, Ma Island and that extraordinary sunset at Amsterdam Bar.

Then Koh Tao gave us the chance to slow everything down, wander the streets and enjoy some time on the beach.

Three islands, each with its own personality. Together, they made a wonderful trip.
