THAILAND

Island Life

Three islands, endless beaches and a slower rhythm of life — exploring Koh Samui, Koh Tao and Koh Phangan.

Begin Journey

The Experience

Three islands, one escape

Thailand's islands have a way of making you slow down.

We spent time across Koh Samui, Koh Tao and Koh Phangan, and each island had its own personality. Some days were about finding a beautiful beach and staying there for hours. Others were about getting out on the water, exploring the coastline or simply finding somewhere relaxed for lunch.

We stayed in a selection of hotels along the way. Each gave us a different base for island life, but the common theme was the same — somewhere comfortable to come back to after a day in the sun.

The best part was not having to do too much. A beach, warm water, a good lunch and nowhere particular to be was often enough.

01

Koh Samui

Koh Samui was our introduction to this stretch of Thailand's islands. It has everything you want from a tropical escape — beautiful beaches, lush hills, warm water and plenty of places to eat and relax.

It also has enough going on that you can easily mix quiet beach days with exploring, restaurants and evenings out.

02

Koh Tao

Koh Tao felt more rugged and adventurous.

The coastline, rocky headlands and clear water make exploring the island particularly rewarding. Some of the best moments came simply from finding a viewpoint, looking out across the sea and taking in how beautiful the surroundings were.

03

Koh Phangan

Koh Phangan has a different energy again.

Away from its famous party reputation, there are beautiful beaches, quiet stretches of coastline and plenty of places where island life feels wonderfully uncomplicated.

For us, it was another chance to slow down and enjoy the surroundings.

Where We Stayed

Our island bases

We stayed in a selection of hotels across the three islands, and each gave us a slightly different version of island life.

The best ones had the things that matter on a beach holiday — comfortable rooms, good pools, easy access to the water and somewhere enjoyable to spend time when we weren't exploring.

Hotel notes

We can add the individual hotel names, room types, family facilities and our ratings here once we've polished the details.

For now, the important thing is that having a good base made moving between the islands easy and gave us somewhere comfortable to come back to at the end of each day.

Island Life

The days are the destination

What we enjoyed most was the freedom to let the days unfold.

Swim in the morning. Find somewhere for lunch. Take a boat out into the blue. Stop for a drink. Watch the light change over the water.

There was never much need for a complicated itinerary.

These islands are at their best when you leave some space in the day.
