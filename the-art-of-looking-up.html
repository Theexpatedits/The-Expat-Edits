The Art of Looking Up

A collection of the details that make a place memorable.

I have always enjoyed noticing the street art, graffiti and little visual details that appear when we're travelling. Sometimes it's a huge mural on the side of a building. Sometimes it's a neon sign above a restaurant, an unexpected painting in a doorway or a small piece of humour that makes you stop and look twice.

They're not necessarily the things you set out to see, and they rarely make it into the itinerary. But they're often the details that stay with you. Over time, I've found myself photographing more and more of them.

This is a growing collection from our travels: a few favourite discoveries, gathered along the way.

01 · The Night-time Edit

When the lights come on

Restaurants, bars and late-night corners have their own visual language. A little neon, a bold sign and an interior that catches your eye.

02 · Walls with a Story

The streets become a canvas

Some of the most memorable pieces are impossible to miss. Colourful characters, portraits and scenes that transform an ordinary walk into something worth remembering.

What I enjoy most is the variety. Some pieces are carefully considered murals, others are playful, slightly eccentric or almost hidden in plain sight. They give buildings and interiors a personality of their own.

And not everything needs to be a masterpiece. Sometimes the best find is simply a sign, a phrase or a small detail that makes you smile.

“The best discoveries are often the ones you weren't looking for.”
03 · The Unexpected

A few details worth a second look

Not every discovery fits neatly into a category. Some are funny, some are curious, and some simply make you stop.

04 · A Different Kind of Sign

Little reminders along the way

Sometimes it's not a mural at all. Just a few words, painted or placed somewhere that makes them impossible to ignore.

Keep looking

This collection will keep growing. There are plenty more streets to wander, signs to notice and walls to look up at. And hopefully a few more unexpected discoveries along the way.
