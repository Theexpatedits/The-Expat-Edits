Vietnam

The Experience

From Da Nang to Hoi An

Central Vietnam offers the perfect balance of relaxation, culture and incredible food. We based ourselves in Da Nang before spending time exploring the beautifully preserved streets of nearby Hoi An, a destination that quickly became one of our favourites in Southeast Asia.

Da Nang provides long stretches of golden sand, luxury beachfront resorts and a relaxed atmosphere that's ideal for slowing down. Just a short drive away, Hoi An feels like stepping into another era, where colourful lanterns illuminate narrow streets lined with historic buildings, riverside cafés and family-run restaurants.

What makes this part of Vietnam so memorable is how effortlessly it combines everything we love about travel. Beautiful beaches, fascinating history, warm hospitality and some of the best food we've experienced anywhere in Asia create a destination that's easy to recommend and even easier to return to.

01

Hoi An After Dark

As the sun sets, Hoi An transforms into one of the most atmospheric towns we've visited. Hundreds of colourful lanterns illuminate the ancient streets while the Thu Bon River fills with small boats carrying glowing candles. Wandering through the Old Town after dark is an experience that captures the charm of Vietnam perfectly.

02

Da Nang's Coastline

Da Nang offers a completely different pace. Long sandy beaches, modern cafés and relaxed beachfront resorts make it the perfect place to unwind between sightseeing. We loved starting the day with a walk along My Khe Beach before exploring everything Central Vietnam has to offer.

03

Beyond The Beaches

Some of our favourite memories came from venturing beyond the coastline. Quiet rice fields, mountain roads, traditional fishing villages and welcoming locals revealed a side of Vietnam that felt authentic and wonderfully unhurried. It's these moments that made us want to return long before we'd even left.

Where to Stay

Hotels I'd Recommend

OUR FAVOURITE

Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai

Without question one of the finest beach resorts in Asia. Set on a pristine stretch of coastline between Da Nang and Hoi An, The Nam Hai combines exceptional villas, outstanding dining and impeccable service with effortless Vietnamese elegance.

ALSO RECOMMENDED

InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

Perched dramatically on a jungle-covered peninsula overlooking the South China Sea, this iconic resort offers breathtaking views, remarkable architecture and one of the most memorable luxury hotel experiences in Vietnam.
