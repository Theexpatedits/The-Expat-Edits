A family escape to one of our favourite places in Vietnam, with a fantastic stay at the Grand Signature and days spent exploring Hoi An's beautiful streets, shops and restaurants.

The Experience

Falling for Hoi An

Hoi An is one of those places that is incredibly easy to fall in love with. It has a slower pace than many of the cities we visit, but there is still plenty happening once you start exploring.

We stayed at the Grand Signature, which was fantastic. It gave us a really comfortable base and was exactly the sort of hotel we enjoy coming back to after a day out.

What we loved most about Hoi An was simply wandering. The shopping, the restaurants, the old streets and the little details around every corner made it a place where we rarely needed a plan.

It is somewhere we would happily return to.

A fantastic place to stay

The Grand Signature was a real highlight of the trip. It was a fantastic place to stay and gave us a comfortable base for exploring Hoi An. Having somewhere we genuinely enjoyed returning to made the whole experience even better.

02

Beyond the old town

Hoi An isn't just about its famous streets. Once you head beyond the centre, the landscape changes quickly and you get a glimpse of a much quieter side of life around the town.

03

Shop, eat, wander

This was probably what we enjoyed most about Hoi An. There was always another shop to look into, another restaurant to try or another street worth wandering down. We never felt the need to rush.
