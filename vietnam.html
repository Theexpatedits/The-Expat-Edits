OUR HOI AN

Hoi An

Old streets, family time & a slower rhythm.

OUR VIETNAM

A lovely place to stay. A town we could happily wander for days.

We began our Vietnam trip in Da Nang, spending a day or two there before heading down to Hoi An. It was in Hoi An that the trip really came together for us.

We loved the town, the atmosphere and the freedom to spend our days exploring without too much of a plan. The Old Town was beautiful, particularly in the evening, and there always seemed to be another shop, restaurant or rooftop waiting to be discovered.

Grand Signature was a wonderful base for the family. The kids had plenty to keep them occupied, we had somewhere comfortable to return to, and the Old Town was only a short hop away.

THE STAY

Grand Signature Resort by M Village.

A brilliant family base, with plenty for everyone and an easy journey into the heart of Hoi An.

OUR EXPERIENCE

A hotel that worked brilliantly for the family.

Grand Signature was a real highlight of our stay. The rooms were great, the team looked after us wonderfully and, most importantly, the facilities worked exceptionally well for a family.

There were three swimming pools, all of which functioned really well, and a restaurant beside the main pool that became a great spot for lunch. It was easy to spend a relaxed part of the day at the hotel before heading back out to explore.

The kids loved having a pool table and a padel court to enjoy, while there was even a small gym for me. It was one of those hotels where everyone had something to do.

The location also suited us perfectly. It was only a short hop from the hotel into the Old Town, making it easy to enjoy the town in the evenings without having to make a big journey of it.

For a family visit to Hoi An, we found Grand Signature a particularly good fit: comfortable, welcoming and genuinely useful for keeping everyone happy.

TIME TO RECHARGE

A proper family base.

We liked having the choice between a day exploring Hoi An and a slower afternoon back at the hotel. With three pools, somewhere to have lunch and activities for the kids, it made the whole trip feel easy.

THE LOCATION

Close enough to enjoy the Old Town.

The short journey between Grand Signature and the Old Town meant we could head in for an evening of wandering, shopping and dinner, then return to the hotel without any fuss.

BEYOND THE HOTEL

Discovering Hoi An.

THE OLD TOWN

A town made for wandering.

Our favourite thing about Hoi An was simply exploring on foot. The streets are full of character, with traditional buildings, colourful details and flowers around almost every corner.

We loved taking our time and seeing where the streets led us.

LEATHER, ART & CLOTHES

Shopping became part of the experience.

We spent plenty of time looking through the leather shops, browsing paintings and having clothes made.

It was never simply a case of going shopping. We enjoyed wandering between the shops, seeing what caught our eye and finding things to take home.

BEYOND THE OLD TOWN

Out into the rice fields.

One day we hired bikes and headed out into the countryside. It was a lovely change of pace from the streets and shops, following the quieter paths through the rice fields.

We even bumped into a buffalo or two along the way.

AFTER DARK

Hoi An comes alive in the evening.

The Old Town at night was spectacular. The lanterns, the warm light on the old buildings and the atmosphere made it somewhere we loved returning to each evening.

ROOFTOP SUN DOWNERS

A drink and a chance to put our feet up.

After a few hours walking and shopping, finding a rooftop for a drink was always welcome.

We loved sitting above the streets, watching the evening unfold and taking a little rest before heading back out.

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

Life along the waterways.

Beyond the Old Town, the waterways and surrounding countryside offer another view of the area.

It is worth allowing time to explore beyond the familiar streets and discover some of the quieter surroundings.

More moments from Hoi An.

OUR EDIT

Give Hoi An time to wander.

We started our Vietnam trip in Da Nang before heading to Hoi An, and it was here that we really settled into the rhythm of the holiday.

Grand Signature was a perfect base for our family. The kids had the pool table and padel court, there were three pools to choose from and a restaurant beside the main pool that worked brilliantly for lunch. I even managed to make use of the small gym.

The Old Town was a highlight. We loved walking its streets, browsing paintings and leather goods, having clothes made and finding rooftop spots for a drink when our feet needed a rest.

And then there was the countryside. Getting out on bikes and cycling through the rice fields, with a buffalo or two along the way, gave us another side of Hoi An.

It was a lovely family trip, and one we would happily repeat.
